# Brand Designer: Examples

Business names below are fictional unless stated.

## Example 1: Bilingual identity for a pre-school
**Request:** "Chinnari Steps (fictional), a Montessori pre-school in Nizamabad. Needs a logo for the signboard, ID cards, the WhatsApp group icon and the website. Telugu and English."

**Key decisions:**
- Platform traits: "patient", "hands-on", "warm but orderly". Mapped to humanist forms, rounded terminals, medium weight, warm palette with one steady dark.
- Avoided the category clichés (graduation cap, crayon, rainbow). Concept: a stack of three wooden blocks forming a doorway, drawn from the school's own materials.
- Confirmed Telugu spelling "చిన్నారి" in writing from the owner. Telugu wordmark set in Mandali, balanced by visual weight with the Latin wordmark.
- 48 px avatar test: the wordmark is unreadable, so the WhatsApp icon uses the block symbol only.

**Output (excerpt):**
```

| Ink #23303B | Chalk #FBF8F2 | 12.7:1 | body, headings |

| Marigold #E9A23B | Chalk #FBF8F2 | 2.0:1 | decorative only, never text |
```

## Example 2: Refresh for a 25-year-old pharmacy mark
**Request:** "Our pharmacy logo is old. Make a new one." Existing mark: a green cross with the shop name in a hand-painted script; customers call it "the green cross shop".

**Key decisions:**
- Decision table: recognised symbol, fails at small sizes and has five colours. Choose refresh, not rebrand.
- Redrew the cross with even strokes and one green; kept the hand-painted feel by commissioning nothing, instead redrawing the wordmark as clean vector with the same proportions.
- Flagged that the green cross may be subject to pharmacy signage rules [Unverified; `compliance-research-assistant` to check the current state rules].

**Output (excerpt):**
```
Refresh rationale: keeps the green cross customers already use to find the shop; reduces 5 colours to 1 green + neutral;
symbol now readable at 32 px (screenshot: tests/avatar-48.png, tests/favicon-16.png).
Open question: confirm whether pharmacy signage rules restrict cross colour or shape.
```

## Example 3: Boundary: "Use this logo I found online"
**Request:** "I found a nice salon logo on Pinterest. Just change the name and use it."

**Response (excerpt):** "I can't reuse that logo: it belongs to someone else, and putting it on your signboard and Instagram risks a copyright or trademark complaint, plus it won't be unique to you. I've noted what you like about it (thin elegant script, single gold colour, lots of space) and drafted three original concepts in that spirit for your salon.
