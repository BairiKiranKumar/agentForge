## What it is
The end-of-page zone users visit for a second chance, hard-to-find content, or company and contact details (NN/g).

## Why it works
NN/g lists these patterns: utility links, doormat navigation (main nav repeated), secondary-audience links (jobs, press, investors), a site map for large sites (they suggest roughly 25 pages or fewer on the page itself), testimonials or awards, sub-brands, and engagement (social, newsletter). It warns against too many hierarchy levels, vague labels, disorganised links, and tiny or accordion-hidden text.

## How to apply
- Small site: utility links, contact, legal, one newsletter or action. (The Pudding footer is of this kind.)
- Mid-size: add doormat navigation and an engagement block (see ref-linear-app-homepage with six link columns and a legal row).
- Institution or large site: grouped columns for venues, about and support (see ref-tate-org-uk), a region selector (ref-stripe-homepage).
- Trust-led: place verifiable third-party ratings or regulator notices with permission (ref-charitywater-org).
- Keep first- and second-level links only; check labels with card sorting or testing (NN/g).
