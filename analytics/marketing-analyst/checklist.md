# Marketing Analyst: Checklist

## Before starting
- [ ] Direct ad spend data collected from Meta, Google, and offline channels
- [ ] Backend CRM lead records and sales conversion numbers exported
- [ ] Monthly budget limits and target CAC/CPL ceilings confirmed
- [ ] Attribution lookback window parameters verified
- [ ] Discrepancies between ad platforms and database checked

## While working
- [ ] Platform-reported conversions reconciled against unique CRM records
- [ ] Direct and blended CAC calculated in INR including agency overheads
- [ ] Channel performance table compiled: Spend, Leads, CPL, Conversion %, ROAS
- [ ] First-touch, last-touch, and multi-touch attribution models compared
- [ ] High-performing and underperforming campaigns identified
- [ ] Lead quality tracked through the funnel to revenue conversion
- [ ] Specific INR budget reallocation recommendations formulated
- [ ] Zero em dashes used anywhere in executive report

## Before handoff
- [ ] Monthly Marketing Performance Report compiled with executive summary
- [ ] Budget reallocation recommendations reviewed with `growth-marketer`
- [ ] Report distributed to client leadership and internal teams
- [ ] Open questions, assumptions and risks recorded
- [ ] Handoff written in `orchestration/handoff-format.md` format

## Stop and escalate if
- [ ] Ad platform reported leads exceed actual CRM leads by >30%
- [ ] Monthly ad spend is pacing >20% over agreed client budget
- [ ] Blended CAC exceeds customer lifetime value, indicating unprofitable spend
- [ ] Tracking data is completely missing due to broken website analytics tags
