# Content Calendar Manager: Checklist

## Before starting
- [ ] Approved backlog or strategy received from `content-strategist`
- [ ] Channels per account and weekly production capacity recorded
- [ ] Approval owner named for each account (the owner; client contact plus the owner for client work)
- [ ] Fixed dates collected: launches, offers, admission windows, events
- [ ] Festival dates verified for the current year (Sankranti, Bathukamma, Dussehra, Diwali and others in scope)
- [ ] Calendar and library location confirmed (Notion, Sheet or local Markdown)
- [ ] Existing calendar and index read; published, scheduled and gap reported

## While working
- [ ] Cadence fits capacity (no more than stated weekly pieces)
- [ ] Lead times applied per piece type; none compressed silently
- [ ] Every item has one owner and one reviewer
- [ ] Every brief passes the gate: goal, audience words, one key message, proof with permission ref, format, language, CTA, due date
- [ ] Repurposing ledger searched before briefing new pieces
- [ ] Conflict checks run: duplicate topics within 7 days, sensitive dates, expired offers, client busy days
- [ ] Offers, prices and dates in briefs have a source
- [ ] Consent recorded for any customer, student or patient photos or names
- [ ] Items idle more than 5 working days flagged and chased
- [ ] Social items handed to `social-media-manager` rather than scheduled here

## Before handoff
- [ ] Calendar covers at least the next 14 days with owners
- [ ] No stale rows (go-live in the past, still unapproved)
- [ ] Approval records name approver, date, channel, link and version
- [ ] Finals filed in the library with the naming convention and indexed
- [ ] Derivatives carry source IDs
- [ ] Assumptions, open questions and risks listed
- [ ] Handoff written in `orchestration/handoff-format.md` format with evidence

## Stop and escalate if
- [ ] A piece is about to go out without a recorded approval
- [ ] A client account has no named approver
- [ ] Requested cadence exceeds capacity by more than 25% and the owner insists
- [ ] A brief requires personal data or images without consent
