# Service Designer: Checklist

## Before starting
- [ ] Brief states what changes in the service, not only which screens
- [ ] Evidence of the current service exists (research, walkthrough, sample messages)
- [ ] Staff roles and peak-shift headcount known
- [ ] Channels in use today listed with who answers each
- [ ] Children, patients or payments held for others flagged

## While working
- [ ] Touchpoint inventory covers digital, human, physical and printed touchpoints
- [ ] Every current-state step cites an evidence reference
- [ ] All blueprint layers filled per step or marked "none"
- [ ] Fail points, wait points and moments of truth marked
- [ ] Fail points rated 1 to 3; rating 3 items have prevention and recovery
- [ ] Each channel switch states what travels (ID, name) and maximum wait
- [ ] The decision-maker who pays (parent, spouse, owner) has their touchpoints mapped
- [ ] Language and literacy considered (voice notes, Telugu or Hindi, icons plus text)
- [ ] Staff load checked per role at peak, today versus future
- [ ] Busiest role gains no net work, or trade-off flagged
- [ ] Each critical-path digital step has a fallback and an owner who switches to it
- [ ] Personal data crossing channels listed for privacy review

## Before handoff
- [ ] Future-state blueprint and service change list complete
- [ ] Screens needed per frontstage step listed for `ux-designer`
- [ ] Backstage rules and states listed for `business-analyst`
- [ ] New staff procedures listed for `sop-writer`
- [ ] Assumptions, open questions and risks listed
- [ ] Handoff written in `orchestration/handoff-format.md` format with evidence

## Stop and escalate if
- [ ] Asked to draw a current state with no evidence and no walkthrough possible
- [ ] Design requires changing staff roles or hours without client agreement
- [ ] A channel customers rely on would be removed
