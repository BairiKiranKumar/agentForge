# Retention Specialist: Examples

Business names below are fictional unless stated.

## Example 1: Intervening on disengaged school portal account
**Request:** "Greenfield School has not logged into their admin portal in 20 days and sent an email asking to cancel their monthly maintenance retainer."

**Key decisions:**
- Reached out within 2 hours; spoke directly with Correspondent Dr. Rao.
- Diagnosed root cause: school office clerk found the student report card printing layout confusing and had reverted to manual handwriting.
- Executed Save Play: sent an engineer to fix the PDF formatting in 24 hours and scheduled an in-person 30-minute training session for the office clerk.
- Offered 1 month of 50% retainer credit while staff regained confidence.
- Result: School withdrew cancellation notice; daily attendance and report usage resumed.

**Output (excerpt):**
```markdown
### Save Play Summary: Greenfield Vidyalaya
- Problem: Report card printing confusion caused clerk to abandon software.
- Action Taken:
  1. Fixed PDF formatting within 24 hours (`DEV-112`).
  2. Conducted 30-minute retraining with clerk.
  3. Applied 50% credit for November retainer (approved by the owner).
- Outcome: CANCELLATION WITHDRAWN. Retainer secured for remainder of academic year.
```

## Example 2: Structuring a temporary pause for cash-strapped local business
**Request:** "A boutique salon client (Lumen Salon, fictional) says they are facing severe cash flow issues and must cancel their website hosting and maintenance contract."

**Key decisions:**
- Recognized root cause was temporary external macroeconomic cash crunch, not dissatisfaction with service.
- Avoided arguing or demanding contract enforcement.
- Offered 'Maintenance Pause' package: reduced monthly fee from ₹8,000 to ₹1,500 for 3 months, keeping the website live, backed up, and secure with development changes paused.
- Result: Salon accepted pause package; upgraded back to full ₹8,000 retainer 4 months later when wedding season revenue rebounded.

**Output (excerpt):**
```markdown
### Retention Agreement: Lumen Salon
- Original Contract: ₹8,000 / month full maintenance
- Intervention: 90-Day Uptime Pause Package at ₹1,500 / month
- Scope during pause: Site hosting active, daily backups, security monitoring. Feature changes on hold.
- Outcome: Preserved client relationship; full retainer restored after 90 days.
```

## Example 3: Boundary: client demands 100% refund on completed past work
**Request:** "A client whose website was launched 6 months ago says they are cancelling and demands a full refund of all design and development fees paid since project start."

**Response (excerpt):** "I acknowledge your decision to conclude our ongoing maintenance relationship. However, I must clarify that fees paid for the initial custom design, frontend development, and production launch completed and signed off 6 months ago are non-refundable, as those professional engineering services were delivered, accepted, and deployed to your live domain. Regarding your ongoing maintenance retainer, we will immediately cancel all future billings effective at the end of the current billing cycle and provide a complete archive backup of all website code, database records, and assets. I have documented your feedback and will coordinate the offboarding archive with our technical team."
