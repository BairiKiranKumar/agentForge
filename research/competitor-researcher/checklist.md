# Competitor Researcher: Checklist

## Before starting
- [ ] Decision served and deadline known
- [ ] Our offer and target segment stated in one line
- [ ] Known competitors collected from client, the owner and prior teardowns
- [ ] Publication intent checked (internal only by default)

## While working
- [ ] Set covers direct, indirect, substitute and do-nothing
- [ ] At least one local competitor included
- [ ] Each page captured with URL and access date
- [ ] Observed, inferred and implication kept in separate sections
- [ ] Inferences labelled with confidence
- [ ] Absence written as "not observed on <pages>, as of <date>"
- [ ] No motives stated without a public source
- [ ] Prices recorded as shown with billing period and GST treatment
- [ ] "Contact us" pricing recorded as not public, not estimated
- [ ] Public flows walked at 375 px on a throttled connection, steps counted
- [ ] Language support (Telugu, Hindi) checked and recorded
- [ ] At least 20 recent reviews read per source where available
- [ ] Review themes counted; under 3 reviews not called repeated
- [ ] Suspicious review clusters excluded and noted
- [ ] Positioning map axes are trade-offs the buyer makes

## Before handoff
- [ ] Every gap and threat tied to evidence IDs
- [ ] "Not checked and why" section filled
- [ ] As-of date on the summary
- [ ] Claims safe for publication listed (none by default)
- [ ] Assumptions, open questions and risks listed
- [ ] Handoff written in `orchestration/handoff-format.md` format with evidence

## Stop and escalate if
- [ ] Access needs a fake account, posing as a buyer or contacting their sales team
- [ ] Asked for a competitor's private client lists, staff details or internal documents
- [ ] The teardown is to be used in a public comparison or ad
