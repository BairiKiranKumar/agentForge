# Product Manager: Checklist

## Before starting
- [ ] Idea statement names who has the problem and why now
- [ ] Constraints recorded: build hours per week, budget in INR, deadline
- [ ] User and buyer identified separately where they differ (owner buys, staff or parents use)
- [ ] Existing evidence collected with sources (enquiries, client chats, analytics, prior research)
- [ ] Regulated areas flagged early: payments, health data, children's data, lending

## While working
- [ ] Problem statement includes segment, workaround, cost and evidence source (or is labelled a hypothesis)
- [ ] Assumptions listed under value, usability, viability and feasibility risks
- [ ] Top two or three risky assumptions sent to `research-lead` as research requests
- [ ] Go/pivot/stop criteria written and dated before validation evidence arrives
- [ ] Prioritization method chosen to fit the decision, scores recorded
- [ ] At least three solutions compared for the chosen problem
- [ ] Outcome metric, leading metric, guardrail and viability metric defined
- [ ] Each metric has baseline (or first reading), threshold, source and evaluation date
- [ ] Willingness to pay judged on commitments in INR, not hypothetical answers
- [ ] MVP cut test applied to every proposed must-have
- [ ] Every must-have traces to a validated need ID
- [ ] Roadmap items written as outcome statements, horizons not dates

## Before handoff
- [ ] Lean PRD stays near one to two pages; no padded or empty sections
- [ ] Assumptions and open questions section lists everything unproven
- [ ] At least three "not doing" items listed for the release
- [ ] Decision memo compares evidence to each pre-agreed criterion
- [ ] Next step cost stated in hours and INR
- [ ] Pricing hypothesis came from `pricing-strategist`, not invented
- [ ] Assumptions, open questions and risks listed
- [ ] Handoff written in `orchestration/handoff-format.md` format with evidence

## Stop and escalate if
- [ ] Asked to write a PRD that presents a decided build as validated
- [ ] Criteria are being changed after the evidence came in
- [ ] Scope involves holding money for others, health or children's data without privacy and compliance review
- [ ] Any spend, launch or customer promise is needed to proceed
