# Customer Success Manager: Checklist

## Before starting
- [ ] Signed statement of work, original proposal, and client goals reviewed
- [ ] Post-launch handover documentation received from `handover-specialist`
- [ ] Primary executive sponsor and daily operational contacts confirmed
- [ ] Analytics telemetry and product login data accessible
- [ ] Support ticket history reviewed for unresolved issues

## While working
- [ ] 90-day Client Success Plan drafted with measurable business KPIs
- [ ] Adoption milestones scheduled with target dates and clear metrics
- [ ] Health score calculated across Adoption (40%), Service (30%), Sentiment (30%)
- [ ] Monthly value digest drafted highlighting concrete business outcomes
- [ ] QBR presentation prepared focusing on ROI (leads, time saved, conversions)
- [ ] Technical jargon translated into accessible business results
- [ ] Expansion opportunities flagged and handed off to `account-manager`
- [ ] At-risk accounts (<60 health score) escalated to `retention-specialist`
- [ ] Follow-up action items assigned with owners and calendar dates

## Before handoff
- [ ] QBR summary distributed to client leadership within 24 hours
- [ ] Health score records updated in CRM tracking sheet
- [ ] Client feedback logged with `customer-feedback-analyst`
- [ ] Open questions, assumptions and risks recorded
- [ ] Handoff written in `orchestration/handoff-format.md` format

## Stop and escalate if
- [ ] Client health score drops below 50 with imminent churn threat
- [ ] Executive decision-maker leaves client company with no successor identified
- [ ] Client demands full refund or contract cancellation due to service failure
- [ ] Team member makes unauthorized commitments for free custom engineering
