# Growth Marketer: Checklist

## Before starting
- [ ] Funnel counts for at least 8 weeks collected, each with source and date range
- [ ] Approved experiment hours and budget from `marketing-lead` recorded
- [ ] Past attempts and their results listed
- [ ] Current positioning and offers read

## Growth model
- [ ] Every stage from attention to won project has a count or is marked `[Assumption]`
- [ ] Constraint stage named with a one-paragraph reason
- [ ] Upstream stages checked for breakage (for example WhatsApp reply time) before choosing the constraint

## Experiments
- [ ] 8 to 15 ideas generated, at least one loop or referral idea
- [ ] ICE scored with stated confidence basis
- [ ] Concurrent experiments within the cap (default 2)
- [ ] Each card has hypothesis with baseline dates, metric source, fixed run period and kill rule
- [ ] Tracking confirmed with `web-analytics-specialist` before launch
- [ ] Page-level A/B ideas sent to `conversion-optimization-specialist` instead of run here
- [ ] Referral design has trigger moment, ask message, reward, tracking and abuse limits
- [ ] Referral data collection limited to what follow-up needs, with consent wording

## Readout
- [ ] Observed numbers reported, not percentages alone
- [ ] Counts under 20 per arm read as directional or inconclusive
- [ ] Effect checked for persistence over 2 periods
- [ ] Failed and inconclusive experiments logged
- [ ] Decision recorded: scale, iterate or kill, with next owner

## Before handoff
- [ ] Model and backlog updated with the result
- [ ] Learning row written in one sentence that changes future work
- [ ] Assumptions, open questions and risks listed
- [ ] Handoff written in `orchestration/handoff-format.md` format with evidence

## Stop and escalate if
- [ ] An experiment would message past clients or customer lists without recorded opt-in
- [ ] A reward or discount would change the rate card
- [ ] Anyone proposes paying, gifting or filtering for reviews
- [ ] An experiment would change a live client site without client agreement
