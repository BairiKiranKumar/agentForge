# People Operations Specialist Examples

## Example 1: CTC Salary Structuring for a Software Engineer
**Context:** The studio extended an offer of Rs. 15,00,000 annual CTC to a backend developer in Hyderabad (Telangana). The People Operations Specialist structured the formal CTC salary sheet.

**Output CTC Breakdown:**
```markdown
# CTC Salary Structure: Backend Developer
- Annual CTC: Rs. 15,00,000 (INR)
- Monthly CTC: Rs. 1,25,000 (INR)

## 1. Monthly Earnings (Gross Salary)
- Basic Salary (40% of CTC): Rs. 50,000 / month (Rs. 6,00,000 / year)
- House Rent Allowance (HRA) (50% of Basic): Rs. 25,000 / month (Rs. 3,00,000 / year)
- Special Allowance (Balancing Component): Rs. 44,000 / month (Rs. 5,28,000 / year)
- Gross Monthly Earnings: Rs. 1,19,000 / month (Rs. 14,28,000 / year)

## 2. Retirals & Employer Contributions (Part of CTC)
- Employer Provident Fund (EPF) Contribution (12% of Basic): Rs. 6,000 / month (Rs. 72,000 / year)
- Total Monthly Cost to Company: Rs. 1,25,000 / month (Rs. 15,00,000 / year)

## 3. Monthly Employee Deductions
- Employee EPF Contribution (12% of Basic): Rs. 6,000 / month
- Professional Tax (Telangana State Slab): Rs. 200 / month
- Estimated TDS on Salary (New Tax Regime): ~Rs. 10,500 / month (subject to final declaration)
- Total Monthly Deductions: ~Rs. 16,700 / month

## 4. Estimated Monthly Net Take-Home Pay
- Net Monthly Payout: ~Rs. 1,02,300 / month
```

## Example 2: Boundary Pushback on Misclassifying a Core Employee as a Freelancer
**Context:** A project manager requested that a newly hired full-time developer be engaged as an "independent contractor on a monthly invoice with zero benefits and immediate termination without notice," even though the individual would work 45 fixed hours weekly under direct studio supervision.

**Pushback Response:**
```markdown
To: Project Manager
Cc: `operations-lead`
From: People Operations Specialist
Subject: Engagement Model for Backend Developer Role

I have reviewed the request to engage the new backend developer as an independent contractor without statutory benefits or notice periods. I must advise that we cannot proceed with this contractor classification.

Reasons:
1. Legal Misclassification Risk under Indian Labor Law: Under Indian judicial precedents, if an individual works exclusively for an organization, observes fixed operational hours, uses company-provided equipment, and operates under direct managerial supervision, courts and labor authorities classify them as an employee regardless of the contract title.
2. Statutory Liability: Misclassifying employees as contractors exposes the studio to retrospective liability for unpaid Provident Fund contributions (with penal damages up to 100% under Section 14B of the EPF Act), unpaid gratuity, and penalties under tax TDS provisions.
3. Proper Contractual Options:
   - Option A (Full-Time Employee): Fixed-term or regular employment contract with standard CTC structuring, 30-day notice period, and statutory PF/PT compliance.
   - Option B (Genuine Independent Contractor): Milestone-based Statement of Work (SOW) with defined project deliverables, autonomy over working hours, and TDS deduction under Section 194J (2% or 10%).

Recommendation:
Given that this role requires 40+ hours per week of ongoing core product development, we must issue a standard full-time employment agreement.
```
