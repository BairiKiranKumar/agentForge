# WF-04: Content Production

Content research, writing, design, review, publishing preparation, analysis.

## Entry requirements
- Topic or goal (awareness, trust, leads), audience, channel(s), deadline.
- Proof available (screenshots, code, results, client permission for client work).
- Authorization: publishing and scheduling live need the owner's approval. Default is drafts plus scheduled-for-review only.

## Roles
`content-strategist` (strategy), `content-calendar-manager` (pipeline owner), `copywriter` or `scriptwriter`, platform specialists (`linkedin-specialist`, `instagram-specialist`, `threads-specialist`, `x-specialist`, `youtube-strategist`, `short-form-video-strategist`), design (`canva-specialist`, `presentation-designer`, `illustrator`), `editor`, `proofreader`, `brand-voice-specialist`, `social-media-manager`, `marketing-analyst`.

## Stages
| # | Stage | Owner | Deliverables | Quality gate | Reviewer |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Brief | `content-calendar-manager` | Content brief (goal, audience, key message, proof, format, CTA) | One key message; proof identified | `content-strategist` |
| 2 | Research | writer (with `seo-specialist` for search-led pieces) | Notes with sources, angle | Claims sourced; nothing invented | `editor` |
| 3 | Draft | `copywriter` / `scriptwriter` / platform specialist | Draft per channel | Platform-native format; hook variants | `editor` |
| 4 | Design | `canva-specialist` or `presentation-designer` | Visuals, carousels, thumbnails | Readable at phone size; brand kit used | `design-reviewer` for high-visibility pieces |
| 5 | Edit and proof | `editor`, then `proofreader` | Final copy | Voice rules (`brand-voice-specialist` spot check), no em dashes, facts checked | `brand-voice-specialist` |
| 6 | Publish prep | `social-media-manager` | Calendar entries, posts in draft or for-review state, alt text, links | Nothing live without approval | Owner approves |
| 7 | Analysis | `marketing-analyst` | Performance note after 7 days | Uses actual platform data; compares to baseline | `content-strategist` |

## Smallest-team rules
- Single Threads post: `threads-specialist` drafts, `proofreader` checks. Two roles.
- Carousel: platform specialist, `canva-specialist`, `editor`.

## Exit criteria
Approved assets ready in the calendar (or published by the owner) and a performance note filed in the content library.

## Example invocation
"Run WF-04 for a carousel and a Threads post about how the school portal handles fee receipts. Draft only."
