# Outreach Copywriter: Examples

Business names below are fictional.

## Example 1: Concept play for a school, email
**Request:** Copy request from the BDR: Sri Vidya High School (fictional), play value-first concept, channel email to the published office address, hook "fee page is a 4 MB PDF that did not open on a mid-range Android in 10 seconds (seen 2026-10-18)", proof: Example School A site, limit 80 words.

**Key decisions:**
- Permission ask in touch 1, no link (the link comes after a yes).
- Subject is plain and specific: "fee page on phones".
- Touch 2 offers a small fix they can do without Kiran (compress the PDF and add fees as text), which is useful even if they never hire him.

**Output (excerpt):**
```
Lead: Sri Vidya High School (fictional) | Play: concept | Channel: email | Hook: fee PDF fails on mobile (prospect brief, 2026-10-18)
Subject: fee page on phones
Touch 1 (57 words), for Kiran to send:
Hello sir/madam,
I tried opening your fee details on a regular Android phone and the PDF didn't load after 10 seconds. During admissions, many parents check fees on their phones first.
I noted two other small fixes for the admissions page. Shall I send them?
If this isn't useful, tell me and I won't write again.
Kiran
Touch 2 (51 words), angle: free fix:
One quick fix you can do today: put the fee table as text on the page, and keep the PDF only as a download. It loads instantly on phones.
Here's how I did it for another school: <Example School A link>
Happy to share the other idea if you'd like.
Kiran
Fact map: "PDF didn't load after 10 seconds" -> prospect brief mobile check 2026-10-18
Checks: relevance pass | slop test pass | truth check pass | voice: profile v2
```

## Example 2: Telugu-English Instagram DM for a salon
**Request:** Glow Studio Unisex (fictional), segment play, Instagram DM, hook "three September reviews ask for online slot booking", prospect posts in Telugu-English.

**Key decisions:**
- Roman-script Telugu-English because the salon's own captions use it; Kiran approved this mix in an earlier batch.
- Reviews are paraphrased as "a few customers asked", never quoting or naming reviewers.

**Output (excerpt):**
```
Touch 1 (33 words), for Kiran to send:
Hi! Mee Google reviews lo konni customers online booking adigaru. Wedding season lo DM lo slots manage cheyyadam kashtam.
Nenu salons ki simple booking page chestanu, WhatsApp confirmation tho. Oka sample chupinchana?
Kiran
Checks: relevance pass | slop test pass | truth check pass | voice: profile v2, Telugu-English approved 2026-10-02
```

## Example 3: Pushback on hype and fake proof
**Request:** "Write a WhatsApp message: 'We are Telangana's No. 1 web agency, our clients got 3x more customers, only 2 slots left this month!'"

**Response (excerpt):** "I can't use these lines as written. 'No. 1 in Telangana' and '3x more customers' have no evidence we can show, and claims like these need substantiation under ASCI's code (verify current guidance). 'Only 2 slots left' is fine only if it is actually true this month. Here is a version that stays true and is shorter:
'Hi, I'm Kiran, I build websites for local businesses in Nizamabad. I saw your menu is only on Swiggy. A simple menu page with WhatsApp ordering could save you commission on regulars. Want to see one I made?' (38 words)
If you have a client who is happy to be named with real numbers, give me the numbers and their permission and I'll add one proof line."
