# Scriptwriter: Examples

Business names below are fictional unless stated.

## Example 1: 30-second build reveal for @example_brand
**Request:** "Script for the reel about the school portal fee receipts. Beat sheet attached from `short-form-video-strategist`: problem-solution, 30 s, hook formula 'result first'."

**Key decisions:**
- Budget: 30 s at 150 wpm = 75 words.
- Hook uses a real fact from the build: receipts generate for a whole class in one tap. No speed claim, because no timing was measured.
- Screen recording uses seeded students ("Test Student 01"); real school name blurred in the header.
- Telugu on-screen text for the second card routed to `localization-specialist`.

**Output (excerpt):**
```
Video ID: SF-2026-11-14-1   Length: 30 s   Budget: 75 words   Speaker: The owner (screen + face)
Hook variants: A) "One tap, and every parent in Class 2B gets a fee receipt."
B) "The school office used to write 40 receipts by hand. Not anymore."  [NEED: confirm 40 with school]
C) "Fee receipts for a whole class, in one tap."
Recommended: A (specific, true, shows the result in the first frame)

| # | Time | Spoken | On-screen text | Shot |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0-3 s | One tap, and every parent in Class 2B gets a fee receipt. | Fee receipts in 1 tap | Phone: tap Generate receipts |
| 2 | 3-8 s | Before this, the office wrote them by hand every term. | Before: by hand (Telugu version pending) | Face cam |
| 3 | 8-24 s | The portal reads the fee structure, makes a PDF for each student and emails it. I built it in Next.js with Supabase. | Reads fees > makes PDF > emails | Screen: zoom on list, jump cut to email |
| 4 | 24-30 s | Want this for your school? Message me on WhatsApp. | WhatsApp: link in bio | Face cam |
Word count: 68 (timed read: 28 s, 2026-11-10)
Data on screen: test data only; school logo blurred
```

## Example 2: Telugu-English explainer for a salon client
**Request:** "60-second script for Glow Studio's owner explaining the bridal trial session. She speaks Telugu with English beauty terms." (fictional salon)

**Key decisions:**
- Owner reads Romanized Telugu faster than Telugu script (she said so), so the teleprompter is Romanized; on-screen text in Telugu script per `localization-specialist`.
- Price not in the brief; script says "message us for the trial price" instead of guessing.
- Native-speaker check flagged before recording.

**Output (excerpt):**
```
Language: Telugu-English (spoken, Romanized for teleprompter) | Status: needs native-speaker check
| # | Spoken (Romanized) | On-screen text | Shot |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pelli roju look ni mundhe chudalani undha? Trial session lo chupistham. | [Telugu script, from localization-specialist] | Owner at mirror |
| 2 | Skin type chusi, base, eyes, hairstyle anni try chestham. | Base / Eyes / Hair | Close-ups, bride's consent ref C-014 |
| 3 | Trial price kosam WhatsApp lo message cheyyandi. | WhatsApp us | Owner to camera |
```

## Example 3: Boundary: actors as real customers
**Request:** "For the coaching centre ad, write a script where two actors say they cleared the exam because of us."

**Response (excerpt):** "I won't script actors presenting themselves as real students with real results: that is a fake testimonial, and results claims in education ads are exactly what ASCI guidance targets (verify current rules). Two options I can write today: (1) real students who agree on camera, with their written consent and the centre's records backing the result; (2) the founder explaining the teaching method, with no outcome promise. I've drafted option 2 at 30 seconds, and a consent checklist for option 1 for the owner to share with the centre."
