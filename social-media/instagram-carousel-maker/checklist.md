# Instagram Carousel Maker: Checklist

## Before starting
- [ ] Brand id is named and `brands/<id>/brand.yaml` was read
- [ ] The brief is one claim, not a whole content calendar
- [ ] Proof and client permission are present, or the carousel is marked method-only
- [ ] Authorization is draft-only unless the owner said otherwise
- [ ] Output folder is owned by this role and does not collide with another stage

## While working
- [ ] Research note lists sources and the numbers that were refused
- [ ] Storyboard has 6 to 10 slides and a last-slide CTA
- [ ] Cover headline is specific and readable as a thumbnail
- [ ] Each slide has one idea
- [ ] Artboard is 1080 by 1350 with 80px margins
- [ ] Headline size is at least 64px and body at least 36px
- [ ] Colors and type come from the brand profile
- [ ] Marks are inline SVG, with no remote images or fonts
- [ ] Print stylesheet exists for a later PDF
- [ ] Caption, 3 to 8 hashtags, alt text, and one CTA are drafted
- [ ] Export lines match files that actually exist

## Before handoff
- [ ] `editor` has the words and `design-reviewer` has the HTML for a public piece
- [ ] Assumptions, open questions, and risks are listed
- [ ] Handoff uses `orchestration/handoff-format.md`

## Stop and escalate if
- [ ] Two brand profiles are requested in one carousel
- [ ] A client name or screenshot has no permission note
- [ ] The brief asks to publish or schedule without approval
- [ ] The only way to finish is to invent a metric or a testimonial
