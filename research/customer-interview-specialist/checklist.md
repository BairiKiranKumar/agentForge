# Customer Interview Specialist: Checklist

## Before starting
- [ ] Riskiest assumptions and stage 1 go/pivot/stop criteria received and dated
- [ ] Plan approved by `research-lead`
- [ ] Participants recruited and consenting via `research-operations-specialist`
- [ ] Friends, family or existing clients in the sample declared as bias
- [ ] Commitment ask (if any) approved by the owner, with price from `pricing-strategist`

## While working
- [ ] Every guide question maps to an assumption or criterion
- [ ] No "would you", "how much would you pay" or leading questions
- [ ] Problem not named before the participant raises it
- [ ] No concept or pitch in the problem section
- [ ] Specific last-time stories collected for the core job
- [ ] Workarounds and their cost in INR or time captured
- [ ] Laddered to a business consequence on key pains
- [ ] Buying process captured: decider, approver, payment form, timing
- [ ] Switch interviews rebuild the timeline and four forces
- [ ] Commitments recorded verbatim as yes, no or deferred
- [ ] Notes tagged PAST, WORKAROUND, SPEND, COMMIT, EMOTION, OPINION, HYPOTHETICAL, REQUEST
- [ ] Original-language quotes kept with translation

## Before handoff
- [ ] Summary per interview written within 24 hours
- [ ] Evidence table maps to each criterion with evidence for and against
- [ ] Counts shown as n of N, unprompted and prompted separately
- [ ] OPINION and HYPOTHETICAL lines excluded from criterion counts
- [ ] Willingness to pay reported on the evidence ladder
- [ ] Assumptions, open questions and risks listed
- [ ] Handoff written in `orchestration/handoff-format.md` format with evidence

## Stop and escalate if
- [ ] Asked to sell, quote or promise delivery during a research interview
- [ ] Criteria are missing or being changed after interviews
- [ ] A commitment ask would take money without approval
