# Creator Partnerships Specialist: Checklist

## Before starting
- [ ] Goal and target customer written
- [ ] Budget type and ceiling approved by the owner or the client, with the approval reference
- [ ] Offer facts confirmed by the owner (items, prices, dates)
- [ ] Brand redlines recorded
- [ ] Regulated claims (health, beauty treatment, finance, education outcomes) flagged and routed
- [ ] Minors in content identified; guardian consent route agreed
- [ ] Current ASCI influencer guideline opened on ascionline.in and version date recorded

## While working
- [ ] Shortlist built from audience evidence (comments, location tags), not follower counts
- [ ] Each creator scored on the 5-check scorecard; only 7 of 10 or more shortlisted
- [ ] Engagement compared with the creator's own median, no imported benchmarks
- [ ] Last 3 months checked for brand safety and past disclosure behaviour
- [ ] Outreach drafts reference specific content of each creator
- [ ] Deal options listed without invented rates; creator quotes recorded as quotes
- [ ] Barter and gifted deals still carry a disclosure requirement
- [ ] Brief has why, 2 to 3 true talking points with proof, one CTA, guardrails
- [ ] Disclosure block: exact label, upfront placement, on-screen, spoken, platform tool
- [ ] Tracking set per creator (prefilled WhatsApp, UTM, code, counter question)
- [ ] Usage rights, exclusivity or long terms sent to `contract-review-assistant`

## Before handoff
- [ ] Creator drafts reviewed against brief and disclosure rules
- [ ] Live posts checked within 24 hours; fixes requested and logged
- [ ] Report shows cost per tracked outcome per creator
- [ ] Assumptions, open questions and risks listed
- [ ] Handoff written in `orchestration/handoff-format.md` format with evidence

## Stop and escalate if
- [ ] Anyone asks to skip or hide disclosure
- [ ] A creator wants to make health, finance or result claims without qualifications or proof
- [ ] Payment is requested in advance, in cash without invoice, or for guaranteed views
- [ ] An agreement is about to be accepted without contract review
- [ ] Spend exceeds the approved ceiling
