# Copywriter: Examples

Business names below are fictional unless stated.

## Example 1: Play school homepage hero (Situation A)
**Request:** "Write homepage copy for Little Steps Play School, Kompally. The owner's brief says 'quality education, caring teachers, holistic development'."

**Key decisions:**
- Diagnosis A: the brief is generic. Asked the owner for facts: ages 2 to 5, CCTV parents can view on phone, batch of 15 per teacher, Telugu and English rhymes, van service in 4 km.
- "Holistic development" fails the swap test; replaced with what a parent can picture.
- "Best play school in Kompally" requested; declined with the unlock path.

**Output (excerpt):**
```
Diagnosis: A (generic brief; rebuilt from owner's facts sent 2026-10-15)
Primary action: Book a school visit on WhatsApp

Headline: Play school in Kompally for ages 2 to 5
Subhead: Small batches of 15, CCTV you can watch on your phone, and a van that picks up within 4 km.
CTA: Book a school visit / helper: Opens WhatsApp with "I'd like to visit Little Steps" typed in

| Claim | Source | Status | Unlock evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Batch of 15 per teacher | Owner message 2026-10-15 | supported | - |
| CCTV viewable by parents | Owner message, app screenshot | supported | - |
| Best play school in Kompally | none | declined | Independent ranking or award with date |
```

## Example 2: Restoring a salon owner's voice (Situation B)
**Request:** "Clean up this About text. The owner wrote it herself." Draft: "I started this parlour in 2012 from one chair in my house. Now we are 6 girls, all trained by me only. No rushing, we take time for every bride."

**Key decisions:**
- Diagnosis B: real voice, a clear story and opinion ("no rushing"). Keep "parlour", "trained by me", the 2012 origin.
- Fixed only grammar and order; did not add "passionate beauty professionals".

**Output (excerpt):**
```
Diagnosis: B (owner's story and words "parlour", "trained by me", "no rushing" kept)

I started this parlour in 2012 with one chair in my house. Today there are six of us, and I have trained every one myself. We do not rush. Every bride gets the time her look needs, from the trial to the final pin.

Preserved phrases: parlour, trained by me, we do not rush (from "no rushing")
Changed: tense and word order only
```

## Example 3: Boundary: a clinic wants a guarantee
**Request:** "Landing page headline for a physiotherapy clinic: 'Guaranteed pain relief in 3 sessions or your money back'."

**Response (excerpt):** "I haven't written this. A guaranteed health outcome is a claim the clinic would have to prove for every patient, and health claims are high risk under ASCI guidance (verify current rules). If the clinic really runs a refund policy, I need the written policy first, and the owner and the clinic should review it. Closest supportable headline from what I have: 'Physiotherapy in Banjara Hills, first assessment in 45 minutes', using the session length from your schedule. Unlock evidence for a stronger claim: anonymised outcome records with dates, or a signed refund policy."
