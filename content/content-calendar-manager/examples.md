# Content Calendar Manager: Examples

Business names below are fictional unless stated.

## Example 1: November calendar for @example_brand
**Request:** "Turn the Q4 backlog into a November calendar. I can film two reels and write one long piece every two weeks."

**Key decisions:**
- Opened with the standing-calendar report; found a gap from 7 November.
- Capacity allowed 3 social pieces a week plus 2 long pieces in the month; dropped 4 backlog items to December rather than compressing lead times.
- The school portal build breakdown became the flagship; carousel, reel and Threads post logged as derivatives with source ID KBA-041.
- Diwali week left free of promotional posts, verified date noted.

**Output (excerpt):**
```
Since last review: 5 published, 3 scheduled (to 2026-11-06), gap from 2026-11-07
| ID | Go-live | Channel | Format | Title | Lang | Owner | Reviewer | State | Source ID |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| KBA-041 | 2026-11-10 | Site | Guide | Fee receipts in a school portal | EN | copywriter | editor | briefed | - |
| KBA-042 | 2026-11-12 | Instagram | Carousel | Fee receipt flow in 6 screens | EN | instagram-specialist | editor | assigned | KBA-041 |
| KBA-043 | 2026-11-14 | Reels/Shorts | Reel | 30-second build reveal | EN + Telugu captions | scriptwriter | proofreader | assigned | KBA-041 |
Moved to December (capacity): KBA-047, KBA-048, KBA-050, KBA-051
```

## Example 2: Brief and approval for a restaurant client
**Request:** "Plan the Sankranti menu post for the restaurant site and Instagram." (fictional Annapurna Family Restaurant)

**Key decisions:**
- Price list for the festival thali was missing from the client's message; brief marks it `[NEED: price from owner]` instead of using last year's.
- Approval owner is the restaurant manager plus the owner; manager's approval of version 1 did not cover version 2 with a changed photo, so approval was re-requested.

**Output (excerpt):**
```
ID / Title: ANN-012 Sankranti special thali
Goal and single action: Table bookings for 14 to 16 January; tap "Book on WhatsApp"
Audience and problem: Families in Kukatpally who want a festival meal without cooking at home
Key message: Our Sankranti thali is served 14 to 16 January, book a table on WhatsApp
Proof: Owner's thali photo (consent not needed, no people); price [NEED: price from owner]
Format and channels: Site banner + Instagram carousel + WhatsApp status image
Language: English and Telugu
Approval ref: v1 approved by manager, WA 2026-12-20 10:42; v2 (new photo) re-sent 2026-12-21, pending
```

## Example 3: Boundary: "Just post it, the client said OK"
**Request:** "The client said OK on WhatsApp, go ahead and schedule all ten posts live for this week."

**Response (excerpt):** "I can't schedule anything live; that needs the owner's go-ahead and it runs through `social-media-manager`. I checked the client's message: the 'OK' was sent on 3 November for the draft carousel only, and six of the ten posts were written after that. I've marked those six 'ready for approval' and drafted a WhatsApp message for the owner to send the client with all six attached and a request to approve each by number."
