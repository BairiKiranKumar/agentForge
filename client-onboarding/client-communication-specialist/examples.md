# Client Communication Specialist: Examples

Business names below are fictional unless stated.

## Example 1: Weekly status report for school portal build
**Request:** "Draft the Week 2 progress update for Pragathi School Portal to send to Dr. Rao."

**Key decisions:**
- Collected completed items: mobile homepage layout and admissions enquiry form prototype.
- Set upcoming tasks: noticeboard announcements and staff gallery.
- Identified client dependency: high-resolution campus photos are required by Thursday to avoid design delays.
- Structured in clear 3-part format with preview link.

**Output (excerpt):**
```markdown
Subject: Pragathi School Portal: Weekly Progress Update (Week 2)

Dear Dr. Rao and Smt. Shanti,

Here is our weekly progress update for the Pragathi School Web Portal:

1. What We Completed This Week:
- Mobile-First Home Page: Responsive layout complete. Preview here: https://pragathi-preview.vercel.app
- Admissions Form Prototype: Built the multi-step enquiry form with phone validation.
- WhatsApp Notifications: Connected automated enquiry alerts to the school office number.

2. What We Are Working on Next Week:
- Parent Noticeboard: Building the announcements page for circulars.
- Campus Facility Showcase: Designing the interactive photo gallery.
- Mobile Speed Optimization: Tuning images for mid-range smartphones over 4G.

3. What We Need From You:
- Campus Photos: Please share high-resolution campus photos via Google Drive by Thursday, October 24.
- Form Testing: Please test the admissions form on your mobile phone and share any feedback by Friday, October 25.

Thank you, and we look forward to our weekly demo on Thursday at 16:00 IST.

Warm regards,
Kiran
```

## Example 2: Sensitive message regarding delayed client photos
**Request:** "Draft a message to Dr. Sharma of Ayush Clinic. Launch is in 4 days, but he hasn't sent doctor profile photos and bio text."

**Key decisions:**
- Applied the SBI-C framework (Situation, Fact, Impact, Choices).
- Avoided accusatory tone ("You didn't send photos"); stated fact objectively.
- Outlined 2 constructive choices: launch on time with clean placeholder graphics, or move launch date by 3 days.

**Output (excerpt):**
```markdown
Subject: Launch Update for Ayush Clinic Portal: Profile Photos

Dear Dr. Sharma,

We are putting the final touches on your website build, and the appointment booking system is fully tested and ready.

To launch the doctor profiles with full photography on Monday as planned, our team needs the finalized doctor photos and bios by today at 17:00 IST.

If the photos need more time to prepare, here are two constructive options:
- Option A (Recommended): We launch on Monday with clean, professional specialty icons for each department. We can swap in the doctor photos in an update next week as soon as they are ready.
- Option B: We reschedule our public launch to Thursday, October 30, giving your team extra time to finalize the photography so we launch with full photos on Day 1.

Please let us know your preferred choice by tomorrow morning so we can prepare accordingly.

Warm regards,
Kiran
```

I have documented the customer loyalty rewards feature under our Phase 2 enhancements roadmap. After we go live successfully this week, I will prepare a dedicated proposal and timeline for adding loyalty points. Let us know if you would like to review that next Monday."
