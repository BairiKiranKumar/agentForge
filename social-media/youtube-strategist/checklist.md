# YouTube Strategist: Checklist

## Before starting
- [ ] Channel and goal confirmed (client leads, authority, ad revenue, community)
- [ ] Studio export received: 90 days of views, impressions CTR, average view duration, retention graphs, traffic sources, search terms
- [ ] Missing data listed explicitly instead of estimated
- [ ] Kiran's weekly filming and editing capacity recorded
- [ ] Audience language and geography noted (Telugu, English, mixed)
- [ ] Proof available per planned topic identified

## While working
- [ ] Channel promise written as one sentence and tested against the last 6 thumbnails
- [ ] Every backlog topic scored with named demand evidence
- [ ] Competitor examples chosen as outliers against their own channel median, not raw views
- [ ] Language decided per video; title language matches spoken language
- [ ] 3 or more title options, payoff or keyword in the first ~60 characters
- [ ] 2 or more thumbnail concepts, 3 words of text or fewer, one focal subject
- [ ] Title and thumbnail do not repeat each other
- [ ] Opening 30 seconds shows the promised result
- [ ] Chapters: 00:00 first, at least 3, each at least 10 seconds
- [ ] Payoff placed before the last 20% of runtime; end screen points to a specific next video
- [ ] Retention patterns diagnosed against the channel's own median
- [ ] A/B test plan uses YouTube's test feature without mid-test changes
- [ ] Platform facts cited with as-of date (YouTube Help)

## Before handoff
- [ ] Telugu titles and descriptions sent to `localization-specialist`
- [ ] Thumbnail concepts sent to `canva-specialist`; outline to `scriptwriter`
- [ ] Short-worthy moments sent to `short-form-video-strategist`
- [ ] Paid promotion or affiliate links flagged for disclosure
- [ ] Assumptions, open questions and risks listed
- [ ] Handoff written in `orchestration/handoff-format.md` format with evidence

## Stop and escalate if
- [ ] A title or thumbnail would promise something the video does not show
- [ ] A video features clients, students, patients or employer work without permission
- [ ] Footage or music rights are unclear
- [ ] Asked to buy views, subscribers or engagement
- [ ] A sponsorship is offered without disclosure terms
