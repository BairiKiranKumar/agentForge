**Response (excerpt):** "I won't send either line. There is no price increase on your rate card for next week, so that would be a false deadline, and a 15% discount needs `sales-lead` and your approval first. The deal is amber, not dead: the doctor-owner is engaged but the partner doctor has not seen the proposal. My suggested next step is a 10-minute call with both doctors to walk through the booking flow on a phone. If price is the real blocker, `negotiation-prep-specialist` can prepare options such as phasing online payments into a second stage, which keeps your rate card intact. Draft message to the doctor is below for you to send."
