# Client Training Specialist Examples

## Example 1: Non-Technical Administrator Guide for an Indian Retail Store
**Context:** The studio built a multi-store inventory portal for a boutique fashion chain in Hyderabad. The Client Training Specialist authored a step-by-step administrator guide for store managers.

**Output Guide Extract:**
```markdown
# Store Manager Guide: Updating In-Store Stock Levels

## 1. Goal
Learn how to update product inventory quantities when new shipments arrive at your store.

## 2. What You Need
- Store Manager login credentials (`manager@kiranastore.com`)
- Physical delivery challan or packing slip

## 3. Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Log In: Open `portal.kiranastore.com` in Google Chrome and enter your email and password.
2. Go to Inventory: Click on "Store Inventory" in the left-hand navigation menu.
3. Select Your Store: Choose your location from the top dropdown (e.g. "Banjara Hills Branch").
4. Search for the Product: Type the product name or 6-digit SKU from your challan into the search bar.
5. Update Quantity:
   - Click the "Add Stock" button next to the item.
   - Enter the received quantity (e.g. `25`).
   - Select Reason: Choose "New Shipment Received" from the dropdown.
6. Save: Click the green "Confirm Stock Update" button.

## 4. Verification Checkpoint
- The product stock number updates immediately on your screen.
- A green confirmation box appears: "Stock updated successfully for SKU #1042."
```

## Example 2: Boundary Pushback on Demands for Free Unlimited Training
**Context:** Following the completion of the 2 contracted training workshops specified in the SOW, the client HR manager demanded that the studio provide 8 additional live weekly training sessions for rotating seasonal staff at no additional cost.

**Pushback Response:**
```markdown
To: Client HR Manager
Cc: `delivery-manager`, `scope-change-manager`
From: Client Training Specialist
Subject: Client Training Program: SOW Completion and Additional Sessions

I have received your request to conduct 8 additional weekly live training workshops for your incoming seasonal store staff. I must clarify our contractual training parameters.

Reasons:
1. SOW Training Scope Completion: Statement of Work #2026-05, Section 6.2 explicitly contracted for two (2) 90-minute live training workshops for core managers. Both sessions have been successfully completed, and your team achieved an exceptional 92% pass rate on the operational quiz.
2. Scalable Asynchronous Training Assets: During our engagement, I created modular, self-paced training assets specifically designed to onboard rotating seasonal staff without requiring live sessions:
   - The complete, plain-language Store Manager User Guide (`admin-user-guide.pdf`).
   - Six modular 3-minute video walkthrough scripts, professionally recorded and hosted in your company portal.
   - The 1-page Quick-Reference Cheat Sheet for daily inventory and order updates.
3. Constructive Commercial Options:
   - Option A (Self-Paced Training): Your seasonal staff can watch the 6 recorded video modules and review the cheat sheets, which takes less than 30 minutes per employee.
   - Option B (Formal Training Addendum): If your leadership prefers live trainer-led workshops, `scope-change-manager` can provide a simple Change Order to deliver the 8 requested sessions at our standard training rate (Rs. 60,000 + GST).

Please let us know if you would like us to issue the Change Order for live sessions or if your team will leverage the recorded video suite.
```
