# Retention Specialist: Checklist

## Before starting
- [ ] At-risk account history, contract terms, and monthly retainer value reviewed
- [ ] Support ticket log audited for unresolved bugs and past complaints
- [ ] Usage telemetry and login trends analyzed over preceding 60 days
- [ ] Stated grievance or cancellation reason documented
- [ ] Discretionary concession boundaries confirmed with Kiran

## While working
- [ ] Initial empathetic outreach initiated within 4 hours of cancellation signal
- [ ] 15-minute diagnostic discovery call scheduled with key decision-maker
- [ ] Active listening conducted without defensiveness or premature excuses
- [ ] True root cause diagnosed: Budget vs Usability vs Lack of ROI vs Executive Shift
- [ ] Tailored Save Proposal drafted offering 2 distinct options (pause, downsell, remedial credit)
- [ ] Engineering or support team engaged to resolve underlying technical blockers
- [ ] Concessions approved by Kiran before presenting to client
- [ ] Win-back door kept open if client insists on cancellation

## Before handoff
- [ ] Saved account transition plan documented for `customer-success-manager`
- [ ] Churn Autopsy completed if account was lost, capturing root causes
- [ ] 90-day win-back reminder scheduled in calendar
- [ ] Open questions, assumptions and risks recorded
- [ ] Handoff written in `orchestration/handoff-format.md` format

## Stop and escalate if
- [ ] Client threatens legal litigation, police reports, or consumer court action
- [ ] Churn is caused by catastrophic database loss or security credential breach
- [ ] Required financial concession would render the engagement unprofitable
- [ ] Client exhibits abusive behavior toward team members
