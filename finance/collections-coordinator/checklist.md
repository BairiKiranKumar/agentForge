# Collections Coordinator: Checklist

## Before starting
- [ ] Current invoice register received from `invoicing-specialist`
- [ ] Receipts matched in the last 14 days received from `bookkeeping-assistant`
- [ ] Contract payment terms, late-fee and pause clauses read per client
- [ ] Agreed contact person and channel known per client
- [ ] the owner's payment plan limits recorded

## While working
- [ ] Possibly-paid check run; matches excluded
- [ ] TDS-sized gaps confirmed before chasing
- [ ] Payer score computed from 12 months (default Occasional under 3 invoices)
- [ ] Ladder step chosen from days overdue
- [ ] All open invoices for a client consolidated into one message
- [ ] One call to action per draft
- [ ] WhatsApp version under 60 words; email subject has invoice number and amount
- [ ] Payment details taken only from the owner's record
- [ ] Banned words scan passed (no threats, legal language, penalties not in contract)
- [ ] Planned send time within 9:00 to 20:00 IST, not Sunday
- [ ] Disputes logged verbatim and ladder paused
- [ ] Payment plans have dated instalments and a written confirmation step

## Before handoff
- [ ] Amounts and invoice numbers match the register exactly
- [ ] Summary table plus full drafts presented for the owner's approval
- [ ] Escalation packs complete for step 5 clients
- [ ] Assumptions, open questions and risks listed
- [ ] Handoff written in `orchestration/handoff-format.md` format with evidence

## Stop and escalate if
- [ ] Client disputes the work or amount
- [ ] Anyone asks to confirm or change bank details by message
- [ ] the owner asks for a legal notice or recovery action (needs a lawyer)
- [ ] Asked to contact anyone other than the agreed contact or post publicly
