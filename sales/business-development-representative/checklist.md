# Business Development Representative: Checklist

## Before starting
- [ ] Qualified batch (A and B) and account briefs loaded
- [ ] Kiran's capacity for sends, follow-ups, calls and visits this week recorded
- [ ] Proof assets listed (portfolio links, concepts, permissioned case studies)
- [ ] Do-not-contact list and last week's board loaded
- [ ] Current WhatsApp policy question raised with `whatsapp-business-specialist` if WhatsApp first touches are planned

## While working
- [ ] Every account has a dated hook and a "why now" sentence
- [ ] Accounts without a public business channel dropped with a reason
- [ ] Play chosen per account with a reason
- [ ] First and fallback channels come from the business's published channels
- [ ] WhatsApp used only where the business publishes it for enquiries, one-to-one, sent by Kiran
- [ ] No broadcast lists, groups, templates or automation tools in the plan
- [ ] Maximum 3 touches per account, none on the same day
- [ ] Send windows match the segment's quiet hours (IST)
- [ ] Sundays and major festival days avoided unless the business messaged first
- [ ] Weekly volume within Kiran's stated capacity
- [ ] Copy request per touch includes play, channel, hook, proof asset and word limit
- [ ] Visit plan avoids peak hours and includes concept, printed page and card with QR

## Before handoff
- [ ] Plan marked "Approved by Kiran: pending"
- [ ] Dropped accounts table filled
- [ ] Replies classified with next action and date
- [ ] Booked meetings handed to `discovery-specialist` with brief, reply text and promises made
- [ ] Opt-outs added to the do-not-contact list and sent to `crm-operations-specialist`
- [ ] Assumptions, open questions and risks listed
- [ ] Handoff written in `orchestration/handoff-format.md` format with `authorization: none`

## Stop and escalate if
- [ ] Asked to message personal numbers or personal profiles
- [ ] Asked to bulk-send or automate outreach
- [ ] Opt-outs or "stop" replies exceed 1 in 10 accounts this week
- [ ] Asked to promise prices, discounts or results not on the rate card or not proven
