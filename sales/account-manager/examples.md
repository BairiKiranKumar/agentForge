# Account Manager: Examples

Business names below are fictional.

## Example 1: School website client, quarterly account plan
**Request:** "Run the account part of WF-09 for Sri Chaitanya Vidya Nilayam (fictional), Nizamabad. Their site launched in June."

**Key decisions:**
- Outcome health green (from `customer-success-manager`): enquiry form used during admissions. Commercial health green: both payments on time, owner and office manager both message Kiran.
- Evidence of intent: in the September review the correspondent asked "can parents pay fees online?"; readiness: a new campus mentioned in a local news post they shared.
- Gallery update requests are refinements within the support window, not upsells.

**Output (excerpt):**
```markdown
| Dimension | Owned today | Possible | Evidence | Score |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Modules | 6-page site, enquiry form | Online fee payment | Correspondent asked, Sept review call (notes link) | 3/3/3/2 = 11 |
| Locations | Main campus | Second campus page or site | Shared news post about new campus, 2026-09-28 | 2/2/3/2 = 9 |
| Services | 30-day support (ended) | Maintenance plan | Office manager sends 2 to 3 update requests a month | 3/2/3/3 = 11 |

## Ideas for this quarter
| Idea | Why now | Who cares | Next step | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Online fee payment | Next term fees due in January | Correspondent | 15-minute call to show a fee flow concept | for sales-lead review |
| Maintenance plan | Updates are frequent and support window ended | Office manager | Send plan options from rate card via proposal-specialist | for sales-lead review |
```

## Example 2: Referral ask after a good month
**Request:** "The salon owner messaged: 'Bookings through the site are going well, my friend asked who made it.' Draft the referral reply."

**Key decisions:**
- Positive moment recorded; commercial health green.
- Ask is to forward a short note, not to share the friend's number.
- Referral reward mentioned only if the policy is approved; currently not approved, so not mentioned.

**Output (excerpt):**
```markdown
Message for Kiran (44 words): "That's great to hear, thank you! If your friend wants, you can forward this note to her. Happy to show her your site and talk about what she needs, no pressure."
Forwardable note: "Hi, I'm Kiran, I built Glam Point's booking site. If you're thinking of one for your salon, I can show you how it works in 10 minutes: <link>"
```

## Example 3: Boundary: upsell to a restaurant with an overdue invoice
**Request:** "The restaurant still owes the last milestone but they're opening a second outlet. Pitch them the second site now while they're excited."

**Response (excerpt):** "I've held this idea. Commercial health is red: the final milestone is overdue and the owner hasn't replied to the last two messages. Pitching new work now usually makes collections harder and can feel like pressure. I've flagged the account to `collections-coordinator` for a polite reminder draft and to `retention-specialist` in case something is wrong with the delivered work. The second-outlet idea is in the parking lot with the promoting signal 'final milestone paid and owner responsive'; when that happens I'll draft the brief."
