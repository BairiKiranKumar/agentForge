# Threads Specialist: Checklist

## Before starting
- [ ] The week's material listed: builds, decisions, prompts, mistakes, sourced numbers
- [ ] Week goal set (conversation, profile visits, product interest, DMs)
- [ ] Product or offer details confirmed by Kiran (link, price, what is included) if mentioned
- [ ] Last week's review read and its one change applied
- [ ] Platform facts table re-checked if older than 3 months

## While working
- [ ] Ideas sorted into post types; 5 to 7 chosen with the weekly rhythm
- [ ] At most 1 product or offer post in the 7
- [ ] Format picked per idea (single, chain, attachment, images)
- [ ] Three distinct hooks per post: claim, question, story opener
- [ ] Each post or chain part counted at or under 500 characters
- [ ] Exactly one topic tag; no other hashtags
- [ ] One idea per post; paragraphs of 1 to 3 lines, not broetry throughout
- [ ] No bait closes ("Thoughts?", "Comment YES", "Let that sink in")
- [ ] No rage bait aimed at a group or named person
- [ ] Links and prices placed in a reply or attachment, not the hook
- [ ] Reply bank of 5 written specifically for each post
- [ ] Outbound reply plan lists conversation types and 10 specific angles
- [ ] Every distribution claim labelled as a hypothesis

## Before handoff
- [ ] Every number has a source and date, or the Proof line says no number used
- [ ] Alt text written for every image
- [ ] Posts checked by `proofreader`; long chains or attachments by `editor`
- [ ] Assumptions, open questions and risks listed
- [ ] Handoff written in `orchestration/handoff-format.md` format with evidence

## Stop and escalate if
- [ ] A post criticises or screenshots another creator or company
- [ ] A take touches politics, religion, caste or a community group
- [ ] Asked to copy another creator's viral post word for word
- [ ] Asked to use reply or posting automation
- [ ] Content would reveal employer or client-confidential work
