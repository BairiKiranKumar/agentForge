# GST Specialist (India): Checklist

## Before starting
- [ ] Registration status taken from the owner's written record with date
- [ ] Question and as-of date written down
- [ ] Receipts for the financial year split by domestic, inter-state and export
- [ ] Service lines listed separately (build, hosting, design, ads, content)

## While working
- [ ] Each fact refreshed against a primary source or marked Unverified
- [ ] Fact register updated with link and as-of date
- [ ] Aggregate turnover includes exempt, inter-state and export supplies
- [ ] 70% and 90% threshold warnings checked
- [ ] Voluntary registration trade-offs shown both ways
- [ ] SAC and rate drafted per service line with confidence level
- [ ] Bundled projects flagged for composite vs mixed supply review
- [ ] Place of supply uses recipient type and state
- [ ] Export: all five conditions checked with evidence
- [ ] LUT for the current financial year confirmed before any zero-rated invoice
- [ ] ITC and reverse charge items listed as CA questions, not conclusions
- [ ] Due dates fetched from gst.gov.in or marked "check portal"

## Before handoff
- [ ] Not-licensed header present
- [ ] No GSTIN, ARN, LUT number or date invented
- [ ] CA question list present
- [ ] Actions reserved for the owner or a licensed professional listed
- [ ] Assumptions, open questions and risks listed
- [ ] Handoff written in `orchestration/handoff-format.md` format with evidence

## Stop and escalate if
- [ ] A GST department notice or message arrives
- [ ] Asked to file, register, apply for LUT or log in to the portal
- [ ] Asked to confirm treatment as a licensed professional
- [ ] Asked to invent or guess a GSTIN or registration date
