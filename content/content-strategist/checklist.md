# Content Strategist: Checklist

## Before starting
- [ ] Business goal for the period written in one line (enquiries, admissions, bookings, authority)
- [ ] Paid services listed from the current rate card or client brief (no invented offers)
- [ ] Audience evidence gathered: enquiry messages, reviews, call notes or sales objections
- [ ] Content inventory collected (site pages and the last 30 to 90 days of posts)
- [ ] Analytics exports or screenshots received, or the gap recorded
- [ ] Proof list made, with client permission references for any client work
- [ ] the owner's or the client's weekly production time known

## While working
- [ ] Each pillar passes all four tests: paid service, real question, proof, 10 or more pieces
- [ ] Audience questions quoted in the customer's words; inferred ones labelled `[Inference]`
- [ ] Need map covers aware, comparing, deciding and using stages per segment
- [ ] Every candidate topic scored on impact 40, fit 30, discovery 20, effort 10
- [ ] Topics with no proof excluded or marked blocked
- [ ] Information-gain sentence written for every searchable topic after reading the current top results
- [ ] Offer content at or below 30% of the month
- [ ] Regional-language need checked (Telugu or Hindi audience) and routed to `localization-specialist` if present
- [ ] Seasonal items checked against the current year's festival and admission dates
- [ ] Each flagship piece has its derivatives planned at creation
- [ ] At least one owned asset (site page, guide, email) in the quarter

## Before handoff
- [ ] Strategy, backlog and audit files saved with the naming convention
- [ ] No traffic, engagement or conversion figure without a cited export and date
- [ ] Audit table gives keep, refresh, merge or retire with a reason per item
- [ ] Top 10 backlog items ranked and ready for `content-calendar-manager`
- [ ] Permission requests listed for `case-study-writer`
- [ ] Assumptions, open questions and risks listed
- [ ] Handoff written in `orchestration/handoff-format.md` format with evidence

## Stop and escalate if
- [ ] The plan depends on client results or testimonials that cannot be verified
- [ ] A client wants health, admission-result or income claims as content themes
- [ ] Retiring or noindexing live pages is recommended (needs the owner's approval)
- [ ] "Audience research" would mean profiling identifiable private individuals
