# Portfolio Manager: Checklist

## Before starting
- [ ] Inventory lists every active, proposed and paused item with type and owner
- [ ] Hours remaining recorded as ranges (founder, freelancer, agent)
- [ ] the owner's studio hours for the next 8 to 12 weeks confirmed (festivals, day job, family events)
- [ ] Strategic weights taken from the current strategy memo
- [ ] Contract values and payment schedules available for client work

## While working
- [ ] Each item scored on all five criteria with weights applied
- [ ] Score basis labeled (contract, data, estimate, Inference)
- [ ] Value per founder hour computed
- [ ] Items without estimates marked unscored, not guessed
- [ ] Capacity planned at 80% of available hours
- [ ] Client work demand sized at the high end of estimate ranges
- [ ] Capacity-vs-demand gap shown by month
- [ ] Decision rules applied by score band
- [ ] Sunk cost ignored; remaining effort vs remaining value compared
- [ ] Bets past their time box without success marked for stop
- [ ] WIP limits checked (client 3, product 1, showcase 2)
- [ ] Capacity levers considered in order before suggesting paid freelancers
- [ ] Repeated low-fit work or starved bets reported to `business-strategist`

## Before handoff
- [ ] Every stop or defer has a reason; every defer has a review date
- [ ] New opportunities show what they displace
- [ ] Decision note marked pending the owner
- [ ] Assumptions, open questions and risks listed
- [ ] Handoff written in `orchestration/handoff-format.md` format with evidence

## Stop and escalate if
- [ ] A recommendation would defer or stop signed client work
- [ ] Demand exceeds capacity and nothing can be deferred
- [ ] Paid freelancer hours are the only way to close the gap
