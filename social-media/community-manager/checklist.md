# Community Manager: Checklist

## Before starting
- [ ] Inbox items loaded with platform, link or ID, and time received (IST)
- [ ] Current offer and availability note from the owner on hand (no rate card, no price in replies)
- [ ] Voice notes read; languages the account replies in are confirmed (English, Telugu, Hindi)
- [ ] For client accounts: business hours, owner hand-off number and allowed topics known
- [ ] Previous queue's open items carried forward

## While working
- [ ] Every item has exactly one category from the triage table
- [ ] Complaints, abuse, legal, health or safety claims are pulled to the top as escalations
- [ ] Lead signals checked (one hard or two soft) before categorizing anything as praise or question
- [ ] Each draft follows A-S-K and quotes or references a specific detail from the comment
- [ ] Draft language and script match the person's message
- [ ] No ₹ amount, discount, deadline or result appears unless the owner supplied it
- [ ] No more than one shared sentence between consecutive drafts
- [ ] Disagreement threads get at most one substantive reply
- [ ] Personal data in public comments flagged for hiding with a DM prompt
- [ ] Moderation recommendations name the rule broken and the ladder step

## Before handoff
- [ ] Reply queue table complete with "needs confirmation" marked where facts are missing
- [ ] Lead routing notes written for every qualifying lead, with their words quoted
- [ ] Routing notes contain only business details the person shared
- [ ] Repeated questions (3+ in 2 weeks) logged as content or FAQ candidates
- [ ] Product or site issues handed to `support-triage-specialist`
- [ ] Assumptions, open questions and risks listed
- [ ] Handoff written in `orchestration/handoff-format.md` format with evidence

## Stop and escalate if
- [ ] Someone threatens legal action, claims to be press, police or a government office
- [ ] A clinic, school or food client receives a health, safety or child-related allegation
- [ ] You are asked to send replies, DMs or moderation actions yourself
- [ ] Someone asks you to use tools that auto-comment, auto-like or auto-follow
- [ ] A public complaint is about to be deleted rather than answered
