# Prospect Researcher: Examples

Business names below are fictional.

## Example 1: Salons in the town
**Request:** "Use ICP v2 Profile A. Find 12 unisex salons in the town I could pitch a booking website to."

**Key decisions:**
- Sourced 31 candidates from Maps and Instagram; dropped 19 (7 no activity in 12 months, 4 franchise outlets with head-office sites, 5 duplicates across directories, 3 already contacted per the lead list).
- One salon's only visible number is on the owner's personal Instagram: row kept with channel "Instagram business DM only", personal number not recorded.
- Hooks drawn only from posts and reviews seen in the last 30 and 90 days.

**Output (excerpt):**
| # | Business | Area | Public channels (type) | Presence | Mobile check notes | Hook(s) with date | Source links | Confidence | Seen on |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Glow Studio (fictional) | Commercial Area | Maps phone (business), Instagram business | Social only | n/a | 3 reviews in Sep ask for online slot booking; bookings by DM | Maps listing, Instagram | High | 2026-10-20 |
| 2 | Shine Hair Lounge (fictional) | Greenfield Nagar | Website form, Instagram | Weak site | 360 px: price list is an image, unreadable without zoom; no WhatsApp button; footer says 2021 | Diwali offer post 14 Oct points to the old site | site, Instagram | High | 2026-10-20 |

```markdown
## Glow Studio (fictional), account brief
Fit: unisex, 4 chairs visible, 70+ reviews, active Instagram. Gap: no site.
Trigger: wedding season posts from 5 Oct (Instagram, seen 2026-10-20)
Likely priority [Inference]: fewer missed bookings during peak season
Angle: booking-first website with service list and WhatsApp booking (offer: website)
Proof to use: salon concept from the owner's portfolio
Don't mention: individual reviewers by name; the owner's personal account
Hook found: yes
```

## Example 2: B2B product companies hiring frontend
**Request:** "Find 10 Hyderabad startups that might need React contract help."

**Key decisions:**
- Sources: company careers pages and public job boards; LinkedIn viewed manually only for company pages.
- Freshness: only roles posted in the last 60 days count; 4 candidates dropped for older posts.
- Decision maker taken from the company's own team page; where not published, "Who decides: engineering lead (name not published)".

**Output (excerpt):**
| # | Company | Public channels | Signal with date | Stack evidence | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | LedgerLoop (fictional) | careers@ listed on site | Senior React developer role posted 2026-09-28 | Job post lists Next.js, TypeScript | Medium |

## Example 3: Boundary: request to scrape and collect personal numbers
**Request:** "Just pull every restaurant in Hyderabad from Google Maps with the owner's mobile number so I can blast them on WhatsApp."

**Response (excerpt):** "I won't bulk-extract from Google Maps (its terms prohibit it) or collect owners' personal mobile numbers, and a WhatsApp blast to people who never opted in would breach our outreach rules and risks your number being restricted. Here is what I can do today: a researched list of 15 restaurants in Gachibowli that match the ICP, each with its published business channel, a mobile check of its menu page and a dated hook. `business-development-representative` can then plan a paced, one-to-one approach that you send yourself."
