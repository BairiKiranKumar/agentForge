# Chief of Staff: Checklist

## Before starting
- [ ] the owner's founder hours for the week known (or the 10-hour default stated as an assumption)
- [ ] Fixed calendar items listed (client calls, reviews, recording sessions)
- [ ] Last week's Friday review read; carry-over items pulled in
- [ ] `decision-log.md` scanned for review dates falling this week
- [ ] `commitments.md` filtered to items due in the next 14 days
- [ ] Latest status handoffs from active engagements and the sales pipeline summary collected

## While working
- [ ] No more than 3 must items and 5 should items
- [ ] Each must item meets a qualifying reason (dated commitment, revenue at risk, blocker, costly delay)
- [ ] Each item has exactly one owner and a done-condition checkable on Friday
- [ ] Must-item hours fit within founder hours minus fixed items minus 20% buffer
- [ ] "Not this week" list written so cuts are visible choices
- [ ] Every pending decision has a routing score and an owner from `CAPABILITY-MATRIX.md`
- [ ] Score 3+ decisions have options, cost of each, the lead's recommendation and a decide-by date
- [ ] New decisions checked against active decisions and `do-not-resurface` options
- [ ] Commitments from forwarded WhatsApp messages have a real date, not "soon" or "next week"
- [ ] At-risk external commitments have a renegotiation draft requested from `client-communication-specialist`

## Before handoff
- [ ] Brief under 350 words and ends with one "one thing" plus its reason
- [ ] Missing sources named once at the bottom
- [ ] Approved decisions logged with approver; proposals kept in the pending list
- [ ] Superseded decisions linked both ways
- [ ] Priorities that need a team handed to `orchestrator` with acceptance criteria
- [ ] Assumptions, open questions and risks listed
- [ ] Handoff written in `orchestration/handoff-format.md` format with evidence

## Stop and escalate if
- [ ] A draft would need to be sent to a client, partner or follower
- [ ] Two external commitments collide and one must be renegotiated
- [ ] A forwarded message asks for payment, credentials or bank-detail changes
- [ ] The same blocker appears for the third consecutive week
- [ ] A new instruction contradicts an approved decision without saying "supersede"
