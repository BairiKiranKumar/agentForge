# Partnership Manager: Examples

Business names below are fictional.

## Example 1: Photographer and print shop referral pilot in the town
**Request:** "Find local partners who meet new shops before I do. Start with two."

**Key decisions:**
- Types: referral and bundle. Photographers shoot shop openings; print shops print menus and banners for restaurants that could use a QR menu page.
- Fit scored from Google Business Profiles, public Instagram work and the owner's past experience.
- Leverage for the print shop: their restaurant customers already ask "can you put our menu online?" (customer leverage) plus reciprocal printing work from the owner's restaurant clients.
- Reward left as "to be agreed" because no policy is approved.

**Output (excerpt):**
```markdown
| Partner | Type | Overlap | Reputation | Reliability | Values | Reciprocity | Score | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lens Craft Studio | Photographer | 3 | 3 (public portfolio) | 2 (the owner worked with them once) | 3 | 3 | 14 | Propose pilot |
| Sai Ganesh Printers | Print shop | 3 | 2 (reviews mixed) | 2 | 2 | 3 | 12 | Propose pilot |
| Quick Flex Signs | Sign boards | 2 | 2 | 1 (no reply history) | 2 | 1 | 8 | Light contact |

Pilot (Lens Craft Studio): 8 weeks from 2026-11-01 | Success: 1 qualified referral or 1 joint opening shoot plus site launch | Review: 2026-12-27
Terms summary: referral counted when the photographer messages the owner the business name before the owner contacts it | Reward: to be agreed (pricing-strategist) | Paid after: referred client's payment received
```

## Example 2: Agency overflow from Hyderabad
**Request:** "A Hyderabad agency wants me to build 3 sites a month under their brand. They pay 45 days after delivery."

**Key decisions:**
- White-label overflow: subcontract checklist applied; portfolio rights, IP and 45-day payment flagged for `contract-review-assistant`.
- Capacity check with `resource-planner` before committing: 3 sites a month may crowd out direct clients.
- Pilot of one site first, with go criteria including on-time payment.

**Output (excerpt):**
```markdown
Subcontract review flags (for contract-review-assistant and the owner):
- Portfolio: agency wants no credit; ask for permission to show work privately to prospects
- Payment: 45 days after delivery; propose advance plus milestone, decision by the owner
- Client access: The owner needs CMS and hosting via user invites from the agency, never shared passwords
- Non-solicit clause requested: flag, do not accept as drafted
Pilot: 1 site, go if paid within agreed term and brief quality was workable
```

## Example 3: Boundary: partner offers a contact list and wants to quote prices
**Request:** "The digital marketing freelancer will share her list of 500 business owners and wants to quote our website prices herself to close faster."

**Response (excerpt):** "I've said no to both, and offered a better structure. A 500-contact list shared without each owner's agreement is a personal data problem and would make our outreach cold spam, so referrals should come one by one, with the owner agreeing to be introduced. Letting a partner quote prices means clients get numbers that may not match the scope; the terms say partners don't quote prices, scope or timelines. Instead: a partner kit with a forwardable message and two live examples, and a referral reward (once the owner and `pricing-strategist` set it) paid after the referred client pays. Draft terms are with `contract-review-assistant`."
