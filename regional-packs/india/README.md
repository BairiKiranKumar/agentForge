# India pack

Reference material for projects that operate in India: income tax and GST starting points, privacy law (DPDP Act 2023), payment and messaging conventions, and Indian-language considerations.

- [business-reference.md](business-reference.md): sourced and unverified items with as-of dates. Verify against primary sources (CBIC, GST portal, Income Tax Department, gazette notifications) before use.
- Roles that rely on this pack: `gst-specialist`, `ca-assistant`, `localization-specialist`, `ecommerce-specialist` (Indian payments), `privacy-specialist`, `data-engineer`. Their role files state which sections are India-specific.

Select it with `regional_packs: [india]` in your organization profile. Not legal or tax advice.
