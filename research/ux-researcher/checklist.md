# UX Researcher: Checklist

## Before starting
- [ ] Research brief has decision, owner, date and budget in INR
- [ ] Study plan approved by `research-lead`
- [ ] Recruiting, consent script and storage set up with `research-operations-specialist`
- [ ] Client agreed to contact with its customers or staff
- [ ] Sensitive settings (classrooms, clinics, homes) cleared with safeguards
- [ ] Guide piloted once and fixed

## While working
- [ ] Consent taken in the participant's language before any recording
- [ ] Questions ask about specific past instances, not hypotheticals
- [ ] Participants show the real task on their own phone, register or system
- [ ] Concepts not shown, or only in the last 10 minutes if planned
- [ ] Notes written within 24 hours using Did, Said, Context, Artifact
- [ ] Original-language quotes kept alongside translations
- [ ] Context factors recorded: device, network, language, decision-makers, interruptions
- [ ] Diary prompts sent one-to-one, never in a group
- [ ] Diary check-in done on day 2; over-recruited for drop-off
- [ ] Saturation tracked: stop when two sessions add nothing new, and record it
- [ ] Photos only with permission and without customer faces or records
- [ ] Contradicting evidence recorded

## Before handoff
- [ ] Every finding cites participant IDs and Did or Said
- [ ] Counts as "n of N"; no percentages under 20 participants
- [ ] Job map complete with gaps marked
- [ ] Persona or archetype confidence matches the sample size
- [ ] Limits section names who was not studied
- [ ] Findings submitted to `research-lead` for review
- [ ] Assumptions, open questions and risks listed
- [ ] Handoff written in `orchestration/handoff-format.md` format with evidence

## Stop and escalate if
- [ ] A session would involve interviewing or recording a child
- [ ] A participant withdraws consent (stop, delete their data per the plan)
- [ ] The client insists on attending sessions with its own staff without their agreement
