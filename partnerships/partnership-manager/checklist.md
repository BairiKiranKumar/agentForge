# Partnership Manager: Checklist

## Before starting
- [ ] Current offers, ICP and target segments read
- [ ] the owner's capability gaps and capacity known
- [ ] Existing informal partner relationships listed
- [ ] Referral reward policy status known (approved or none)

## While working
- [ ] Partner types chosen from the type table with a reason
- [ ] Shortlist built from public business information only
- [ ] Fit scored on five criteria with a source per score
- [ ] Skin-in-the-game test answered for each proposal
- [ ] Three-sided value written; shared client benefit is real
- [ ] Strongest leverage identified for each partner
- [ ] Proposed tier matches evidence; no over-tiering
- [ ] Pilot defined with duration, success criteria and review date
- [ ] Terms draft covers referral definition, exclusions, reward trigger and exit
- [ ] Terms state the partner cannot quote prices, scope or timelines
- [ ] Referral consent rule included; no bulk contact lists
- [ ] Reward amounts only from approved policy or marked for the owner
- [ ] Regulated-profession partners flagged for referral-fee rules
- [ ] White-label and subcontract arrangements checked against the subcontract checklist
- [ ] Partner kit includes "what not to say"

## Before handoff
- [ ] Terms marked "draft pending review" and sent to `contract-review-assistant`
- [ ] Reward economics checked with `pricing-strategist` and `financial-analyst`
- [ ] Tax questions on referral fees sent to `ca-assistant`
- [ ] Partner source codes set up with `crm-operations-specialist`
- [ ] Outreach copy requested from `outreach-copywriter`; the owner sends
- [ ] Assumptions, open questions and risks listed
- [ ] Handoff written in `orchestration/handoff-format.md` format with evidence

## Stop and escalate if
- [ ] A partner asks for exclusivity, retainers or minimum volumes
- [ ] A partner offers bulk customer contact lists
- [ ] Asked to sign or accept partner terms
- [ ] A partner wants client data or system access without the client's agreement
