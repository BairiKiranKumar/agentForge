# WF-09: Monthly Account Review

Monthly account review, outcomes report, retention and expansion opportunities.

## Entry requirements
- Active client with a retainer, maintenance plan or recent delivery.
- Data available: analytics, enquiry or lead counts, support tickets, delivered changes, invoices and payment status.
- Authorization: the report and any proposal go to the client only after the owner approves.

## Roles
`customer-success-manager` (lead), `marketing-analyst` or `product-analyst` (data), `customer-feedback-analyst`, `retention-specialist`, `account-manager`, `renewal-specialist`, `collections-coordinator` (if payments are overdue), `client-communication-specialist`.

## Stages
| # | Stage | Owner | Deliverables | Quality gate | Reviewer |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Collect | `customer-success-manager` | Data pack: goals from the success plan, metrics, tickets, delivered work, payments | Every metric has a source and date range | `marketing-analyst` |
| 2 | Analyze (parallel) | `marketing-analyst` or `product-analyst`; `customer-feedback-analyst` | Outcomes vs goals; feedback themes | Comparisons use the same period and definitions | `customer-success-manager` |
| 3 | Health | `retention-specialist` | Health score with reasons, risk signals, save play if needed | Score follows the documented model | `customer-success-manager` |
| 4 | Opportunities | `account-manager` | 0 to 3 expansion ideas tied to client goals, with rough scope | Each idea addresses a stated goal or observed gap; no pushy upsell | `sales-lead` |
| 5 | Renewal and money | `renewal-specialist`, `collections-coordinator` | Renewal date status, overdue invoices and reminder drafts | Amounts match the invoice register | `financial-analyst` |
| 6 | Report | `customer-success-manager` with `client-communication-specialist` | One-page client report and meeting agenda | Leads with outcomes; honest about misses; clear next steps | Owner approves |

## Exit criteria
Report approved and sent by the owner, review meeting held or scheduled, actions logged with owners.

## Example invocation
"Run WF-09 for the school client for October. Draft the report and the agenda."
