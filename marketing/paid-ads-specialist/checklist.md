# Paid Ads Specialist: Checklist

## Before starting
- [ ] Goal result defined in writing (enquiry, WhatsApp chat, call, booking or purchase)
- [ ] Monthly budget in INR confirmed, with whether GST is included
- [ ] Service area defined by radius or pin codes, not the whole state by default
- [ ] Offer, price and expiry recorded with an as-of date
- [ ] Landing page URL live, or the launch held until it is live
- [ ] Regulated category check done (loans, insurance, health, supplements, gambling, political)
- [ ] Client ad account access granted by business manager invitation, not by password

## Tracking
- [ ] Conversion event defined and firing on the test device
- [ ] Platform event count compared with CRM or WhatsApp log for the test period
- [ ] Duplicate firing (form and thank-you page) ruled out
- [ ] Tracking verified before any scale decision

## Campaign build
- [ ] One objective and one audience type per campaign
- [ ] Naming convention applied: Platform_Objective_Audience_Offer_YYYYMM
- [ ] Budget split recorded (test share and proven share)
- [ ] Bid strategy starts with a cap or manual bid; automation only after about 50 conversions in 30 days
- [ ] Budget change steps no more than 20 percent, with a 3-day wait before judgement
- [ ] Negative keyword list created for Google Search before launch
- [ ] Custom audience sources documented with consent basis

## Creative
- [ ] Brief has a hook in the first 3 seconds or first line, with one offer
- [ ] Every claim in the creative has a source listed
- [ ] Vertical (9:16) and square versions prepared for mobile
- [ ] Regional language version planned where the audience searches in it

## Weekly check and handoff
- [ ] Decision made in the order: tracking, post-click, relevance, cost per result, search terms
- [ ] Each decision logged with metric, threshold and evidence
- [ ] Results reported in INR with date range and data source (platform or CRM)
- [ ] Landing page issues routed to `conversion-optimization-specialist`

## Stop and escalate if
- [ ] The client asks to advertise a regulated product without human review
- [ ] Tracking is broken or unverified and the client wants to scale
- [ ] An ad account is suspended or flagged for policy; the appeal goes to the owner
- [ ] A third-party or purchased audience list is offered
