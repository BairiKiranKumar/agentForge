## Example 1: Problem-Proof carousel for @kiranbuildswithai
**Request:** "Make a carousel about how I got the school website to load fast on cheap phones."

**Key decisions:**
- Content is a personal result with a system behind it, so Problem-Proof.
- Proof exists: page weight 3.1 MB to 900 KB, Lighthouse mobile performance 54 to 91 (measured 2026-10-12). School not named (permission covers "a school in Armoor").
- Receipt slide is a real Lighthouse screenshot, not a mockup.

**Output (excerpt):**
```
Carousel ID: IG-2026-10-28-1   Framework: Problem-Proof   Slides: 8 (4:5, 1080x1350)
S1: "School site: 3.1 MB to 900 KB"  | visual: two phone frames, before/after weight
S2: "Parents open it on 4G, on ₹10k phones" | real problem: images, not code
S3: "The system: 4 changes" | list overview
S4: "1. Hero photo as AVIF, 1200 px max" | before/after file sizes
S5: "2. No slider. One photo, one button" | screenshot
S6: "3. Fonts: one family, two weights" | screenshot of font request count
S7: "4. Enquiry form above the fold" | 360 px screenshot
S8: "Lighthouse mobile: 54 to 91" + "Save this for your next site" | real report screenshot
Caption: "How a school website went from 3.1 MB to 900 KB so parents on 4G can actually open it. These are the four changes, in the order they mattered. Save it for the next site you build."
Hashtags: #webdesignindia #frontenddeveloper #webperformance
Alt text S1: "Two phone screens comparing page weight, 3.1 MB before and 900 KB after."
```

## Example 2: Profile and first month for a restaurant
**Request:** "Set up Instagram for Spice Route Mess (fictional), Nizamabad. Owner wants more lunch walk-ins and WhatsApp orders."

**Key decisions:**
- Goal is footfall and orders, so Reels with location tags plus WhatsApp CTA.
- Owner confirmed lunch thali price ₹180; nothing else is priced in posts.

## Example 3: Boundary: before/after for a skin clinic
**Request:** "Post before/after photos from GlowDerm Clinic (fictional) with 'guaranteed acne-free in 30 days'."

**Response (excerpt):** "I won't write 'guaranteed acne-free in 30 days'. Results claims for treatments are a compliance risk under ASCI guidance and health advertising rules, and we have no evidence for it. I've sent the claim to `compliance-research-assistant` to check what is allowed. Patient photos also need written consent for social use. Meanwhile I've drafted a carousel on 'What happens at your first consultation', which needs no patient photos or results claims."
