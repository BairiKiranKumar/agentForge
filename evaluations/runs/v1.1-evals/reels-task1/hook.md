# Hooks

Brand: example-brand. Platform: Instagram Reel, 1080 by 1920. Length: 30 seconds.
Picture: screen recording only. No face, no logo opener.
Structure chosen in this package: unreadable phone hero, then the readable hero that shipped, then the phone check to repeat. No cut plan was supplied by short-form-video-strategist.

Each hook below is the first 1.5 seconds. Seen, said, and on-screen text all start at 0.0.

A) Seen: phone browser, hero headline too small and cut mid-word  Said: "You cannot read this."  On screen: You cannot read this
B) Seen: a wide hero that looks set, then the same hero at phone width  Said: "Looks finished. Unreadable."  On screen: Looks finished. Unreadable.
C) Seen: a thumb beside a tiny headline in a phone browser  Said: "Can you read this?"  On screen: Can you read this?

Chosen: A because example-brand shows the interface mistake first, on a screen the owner actually records, and the unread hero is the decision in this Reel.

## Assumptions
- The broken headline is a demo page the owner controls, recorded for this Reel.
- English, screen plus voice, draft only.

## Open questions
- None that block the package. The owner confirms the demo page before recording.

## Risks
- If the first frame is a logo or a greeting, the hook fails. Start on the unreadable headline.
