# Scriptwriter: Checklist

## Before starting
- [ ] Concept or beat sheet received, or a brief with goal, audience, platform, length and CTA
- [ ] Source material available: build, recordings, code, prompts or client facts
- [ ] Filming setup known: face cam, screen only, phone or laptop, solo or with help
- [ ] Language decided: English, Telugu, Telugu-English mix or Hindi
- [ ] Permission references for any client work or people on screen
- [ ] Word budget calculated from target length and pace

## While working
- [ ] Three hook line variants written, each with a specific fact; one recommended with reason
- [ ] First spoken line is the hook; no greeting before it
- [ ] Spoken track follows the beat sheet structure and timing
- [ ] On-screen text track written separately, 3 to 7 words per card
- [ ] Text track alone tells the core story with sound off
- [ ] Sentences under about 15 words; one breath per line
- [ ] Numbers written the way they are spoken
- [ ] Walkthrough lines narrate why, not the obvious click
- [ ] No real-time typing scripted; jump cuts marked
- [ ] Shot notes match what can be filmed alone
- [ ] Claims checked against sources; no invented numbers
- [ ] Screen recordings use test data; blur list written for anything real
- [ ] Ad scripts: one offer, proof on screen, exact CTA, no superlatives or guarantees
- [ ] Code-mixed lines sound natural; script vs Romanized choice agreed with `localization-specialist`

## Before handoff
- [ ] Script read aloud with a timer; within 10% of budget
- [ ] Teleprompter version produced
- [ ] Zero em dashes; no filler hooks
- [ ] Telugu or Hindi lines marked "needs native-speaker check" until checked
- [ ] Chapter list sent to `youtube-strategist` for long videos
- [ ] Assumptions, open questions and risks listed
- [ ] Handoff written in `orchestration/handoff-format.md` format with evidence

## Stop and escalate if
- [ ] The script would show personal data of customers, students or patients
- [ ] A client asks for actors presented as real customers
- [ ] The script needs a synthetic voice of a real person without consent
