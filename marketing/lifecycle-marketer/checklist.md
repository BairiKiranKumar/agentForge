# Lifecycle Marketer: Checklist

## Before starting
- [ ] Business type and the customer's first action recorded
- [ ] Goal state and expected time to reach it agreed in writing
- [ ] Data fields available listed (name, phone, WhatsApp status, email, consent, service dates)
- [ ] Consent basis recorded for the first message in the journey
- [ ] Offer sheet with prices and expiry dates received, with as-of dates
- [ ] Stop rules agreed: booking exit, unsubscribe, complaint, inactivity

## Journey design
- [ ] Journey map has states, triggers, exits, channels and delays
- [ ] Channel chosen per step with the reason (WhatsApp, email, SMS or call task)
- [ ] No more than 4 messages to one person within 14 days, unless the client approves in writing
- [ ] Sends set in IST and outside late-night hours
- [ ] Branches limited to two or three per journey
- [ ] Each step has one message purpose

## Templates and consent
- [ ] WhatsApp business-initiated steps use approved templates; template status recorded
- [ ] SMS steps only with a registered sender and approved template; regulator rules checked on the current portal
- [ ] Transactional messages kept separate from marketing messages
- [ ] Consent and template register completed for every step

## Briefs and copy
- [ ] Step brief includes purpose, facts with as-of dates, call to action and stop instruction
- [ ] Every message includes a STOP or unsubscribe instruction
- [ ] Regional language versions queued for native review
- [ ] Claims in each step listed with a source

## Measurement and handoff
- [ ] Conversion per step defined, with the window
- [ ] Stop rate per step defined; threshold of 2 percent on one step triggers review
- [ ] Test plan with one variable per step and minimum 100 contacts per variant
- [ ] Reports scheduled at 14 and 30 days
- [ ] Set-up handed to `crm-operations-specialist` or the client with approval recorded

## Stop and escalate if
- [ ] Contact data has no consent record for the first message
- [ ] A step depends on an unapproved WhatsApp template
- [ ] The client wants messages to people who opted out
- [ ] Journey handles health, child or other sensitive personal data without `privacy-specialist` review
