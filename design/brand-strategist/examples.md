# Brand Strategist: Examples

Business names below are fictional unless stated.

## Example 1: Positioning a dental clinic in a crowded street
**Request:** "Smile Point Dental (fictional), Hanamkonda. Five clinics within 2 km. Owner wants to stand out and charge a bit more."

**Key decisions:**
- Claim map: four of five competitors say "experienced dentists", three say "painless" and "affordable". All three marked table stakes.
- 22 Google reviews pasted: 9 mention "explained everything on the screen", 6 mention evening appointments. Neither claimed by competitors.
- "Explains before treating" passes all four tests (intraoral camera owned, valued, distinct, needs equipment and habit to copy). Evening hours fails defensibility (easy to copy) and becomes a support point.
- Rejected "painless dentistry" (table stakes, and a medical claim that needs care).

**Output (excerpt):**
```
Positioning statement: For working families in Hanamkonda who are anxious about dental costs and surprises,
Smile Point is the clinic that shows you what is wrong on screen before any treatment starts,
because every chair has an intraoral camera and the dentist walks you through the images.
Proof points: 9 of 22 reviews mention on-screen explanation (reviews pasted 2026-10-14)
Personality: Straight-talking: does share a written estimate on WhatsApp before treatment; does not say "starting from".
Not this brand: the cheapest clinic; the luxury cosmetic clinic; the "painless" promise
```

## Example 2: Naming a cloud-kitchen sub-brand
**Request:** "Our restaurant (fictional, Annapurna Mess) wants a new delivery-only biryani line on Swiggy and Zomato. Name it."

**Key decisions:**
- Architecture: different product and channel but same kitchen trust; chose endorsed model ("<name> by Annapurna Mess").
- Brief: under 12 characters for app tiles, sayable in Telugu and English, no "royal", "nawab" or "king" (category cliché in the claim map).
- Screened 18 candidates; 4 dropped for meanings or local duplicates; recorded handle and domain checks with date.

**Output (excerpt):**
| Name | Type | Language check | Handle/domain (2026-10-18) | TM screen (2026-10-18) | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dum Dabba | Suggestive | Clear in Telugu, Hindi | @dumdabba taken; @dumdabba.nzb free | 1 similar mark in class 43, review needed | Medium |
| Pothi Biryani | Place/craft | "pothi" understood locally only | free | none found | Low, limited outside Telangana |
Screening only. A trademark attorney must clear the final name before signage or packaging.

## Example 3: Boundary: "Make us the No. 1 school in Telangana"
**Request:** "Position us as the number one CBSE school in Telangana. Put it on the hoarding."

**Response (excerpt):** "I can't build the position on 'No. 1 in Telangana' because nothing we have proves it and ASCI guidance treats unverified superlatives as misleading [verify current ASCI code]. What the evidence does support: your board results are published per student, and parents in 7 of the 15 reviews mention the weekly teacher call. I've drafted a platform around 'the school that tells you every week how your child is doing', with the result data as proof. If you have an independent ranking, send it and I'll check whether a narrower claim can be supported."
