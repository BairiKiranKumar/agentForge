# Chartered Accountant Assistant (India): Checklist

## Before starting
- [ ] Period, purpose and applicable Act (1961 or 2025) recorded
- [ ] Closed books received from `bookkeeping-assistant`
- [ ] Form 26AS / AIS downloads received from Kiran (no credentials requested)
- [ ] Kiran's other income sources as stated by him recorded
- [ ] Prior year computation from the CA located, if any

## While working
- [ ] Books-closed gate run; open items and their possible effect listed
- [ ] Every rate, section and date refreshed with source and as-of date
- [ ] Unverified items shown as "pending verification", not filled from memory
- [ ] Receipts ex-GST; cash share computed
- [ ] Presumptive limit check (Rs. 50 lakh, or Rs. 75 lakh if cash at most 5%)
- [ ] Section 58(4) effect on expense claims noted
- [ ] Presumptive and regular computations shown side by side, no recommendation
- [ ] TDS matched to 26AS / AIS with status per deduction
- [ ] Advance tax net of TDS; Rs. 10,000 threshold checked
- [ ] Instalment dates and cumulative percentages cited with as-of date
- [ ] Profession vs business classification listed as a CA question
- [ ] Arithmetic shown line by line

## Before handoff
- [ ] "Prepared for review by your Chartered Accountant. Not tax advice." header present
- [ ] No PAN, TAN, challan or acknowledgement number invented
- [ ] CA packet complete with documents list
- [ ] Assumptions, open questions and risks listed
- [ ] Handoff written in `orchestration/handoff-format.md` format with evidence

## Stop and escalate if
- [ ] An Income Tax Department notice or message arrives
- [ ] Asked to file, sign, certify or log in
- [ ] Asked to act or sign as a Chartered Accountant
- [ ] Asked to recommend a position the CA has not reviewed as final
