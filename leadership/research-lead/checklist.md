# Research Lead: Checklist

## Before starting
- [ ] Decision, decision owner and decide-by date written in one sentence
- [ ] Prior knowledge gathered (earlier research, analytics, client notes, competitor files)
- [ ] Budget in INR, time and participant access known
- [ ] Sensitive participants or topics identified (children, patients, finance, caste)
- [ ] Request checked for "prove what we already decided" framing

## While working
- [ ] Assumptions rated for impact and confidence; only high-impact low-confidence ones researched now
- [ ] Method chosen from the table, owner role named
- [ ] Sample size stated as a rule of thumb, not a guarantee
- [ ] Study cost proportionate to the cost of being wrong
- [ ] Recruiting uses more than one channel when a gatekeeper selects participants
- [ ] Session language matches participants (Telugu, Hindi, English)
- [ ] Plan reviewed against every approval criterion
- [ ] Consent approach and storage location confirmed before fieldwork
- [ ] No direct research with children
- [ ] Findings separate what people did from what they said
- [ ] Each insight has evidence IDs, implication and confidence
- [ ] No percentages from qualitative samples
- [ ] Market numbers traced to primary sources with as-of dates
- [ ] Contradicting evidence kept and reported

## Before handoff
- [ ] Findings review table complete with accept/reject per insight
- [ ] Limits and what was not covered stated
- [ ] Research roadmap updated
- [ ] Assumptions, open questions and risks listed
- [ ] Handoff written in `orchestration/handoff-format.md` format with evidence

## Stop and escalate if
- [ ] Incentives or paid tools are needed
- [ ] The client has not agreed to contact with their customers
- [ ] A study would record people without consent
- [ ] Health, children's settings or other sensitive topics are involved
