# Business Strategist: Checklist

## Before starting
- [ ] Strategic question restated as one decision with a decide-by date
- [ ] Kiran's constraints known: studio hours per week, cash to invest, non-negotiables
- [ ] Revenue mix and project history gathered
- [ ] Available evidence listed (market notes, teardowns, interviews, win/loss)

## While working
- [ ] At least 3 options including the status quo
- [ ] Segments defined by problem, with current alternative named
- [ ] Beachhead stated: one segment, one area, one offer
- [ ] Segment scores carry evidence labels (sourced, Kiran data, Inference)
- [ ] Contribution per founder hour computed for each option
- [ ] Prices used are rate card values or labeled `pricing-strategist` hypotheses
- [ ] Ansoff position noted for growth options
- [ ] Scenarios (base, upside, downside) for options with large money or time at stake
- [ ] Early indicators named for each scenario
- [ ] Each bet has hypothesis, cheapest test, success and kill thresholds, time box, founder hours
- [ ] No more than 2 bets running alongside client work
- [ ] Concentration, founder-dependence, regulatory and reputational risks assessed
- [ ] Market facts sourced and dated, gaps requested from `market-researcher`

## Before handoff
- [ ] Memo ends in one recommendation with trade-offs
- [ ] Specific "not doing" list included
- [ ] Review date set and decision handed to `chief-of-staff` for the log
- [ ] Reviewed by `marketing-strategist`
- [ ] Assumptions, open questions and risks listed
- [ ] Handoff written in `orchestration/handoff-format.md` format with evidence

## Stop and escalate if
- [ ] An option needs a legal entity, GST registration or contract decision
- [ ] A bet requires spending money or contacting external parties
- [ ] Evidence for the recommended option is mostly inference
