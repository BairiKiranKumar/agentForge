# Short-form Video Strategist: Checklist

## Before starting
- [ ] Idea or source asset in hand (footage list, screen recording, long video with timestamps)
- [ ] Platforms and channel confirmed (English tech, Telugu vlog, gaming, client)
- [ ] Filming capacity known: face on camera or not, time available
- [ ] Consent status known for every person who appears
- [ ] Music plan identified (platform library, licensed, or none)
- [ ] For gaming clips: publisher content policy checked

## While working
- [ ] Structure chosen from the structures table
- [ ] Target length justified by the length table, not the platform maximum
- [ ] Three hooks written, each with visual, spoken and on-screen text layers
- [ ] Hooks filled with a specific fact, place or result, no filler formulas
- [ ] Chosen hook appears in the first second; no logo or intro first
- [ ] Beat sheet has a visual change at least every 4 seconds
- [ ] Real-time typing never longer than 2 seconds
- [ ] Payoff placed before 70% of runtime
- [ ] Captions: 2 lines max, 3 to 5 words per line, safe area respected
- [ ] Telugu text uses a full-glyph font with a post-export rendering check
- [ ] Audio levels noted; music under the voice
- [ ] Clips from long videos get a new hook and start on a complete thought

## Before handoff
- [ ] Spoken lines over 3 sentences sent to `scriptwriter`
- [ ] Cover frame brief sent to `canva-specialist`
- [ ] Platform facts cited with as-of date
- [ ] Series format card updated when the video belongs to a series
- [ ] Assumptions, open questions and risks listed
- [ ] Handoff written in `orchestration/handoff-format.md` format with evidence

## Stop and escalate if
- [ ] Footage shows customers, patients, students or minors without consent
- [ ] Music or clips are copyrighted without a licence
- [ ] A trend involves a stunt or unsafe behaviour
- [ ] Asked to reupload or lightly edit another creator's video
