# Delivery Manager Checklist

## Before starting
- [ ] Review executed Statement of Work (SOW), Master Services Agreement (MSA), and payment schedule
- [ ] Verify baseline financial model and confirm target gross delivery margin (target >= 45%)
- [ ] Confirm team allocation and capacity commitments with `resource-planner`
- [ ] Establish communication channels and bi-weekly executive review cadences with client sponsor
- [ ] Verify that client contractual sign-off authority and GSTIN billing details are documented
- [ ] Review initial work breakdown and effort estimates with `estimation-specialist`

## While working
- [ ] Track weekly team hours and incurred labor costs against contracted milestone revenue
- [ ] Maintain bi-weekly executive communication sync with client sponsor
- [ ] Review sprint velocity and milestone forecasts with `project-manager`
- [ ] Enforce contractual scope boundaries; ensure out-of-scope requests go to `scope-change-manager`
- [ ] Partner with `acceptance-coordinator` to prepare client acceptance packages before milestone gates
- [ ] Monitor client payment receipts and aging accounts with `invoicing-specialist`
- [ ] Intervene immediately when client satisfaction flags or delivery impediments emerge

## Before handoff
- [ ] Secure signed Milestone Acceptance Certificate from authorized client sponsor
- [ ] Notify `invoicing-specialist` to release milestone invoice with 18% GST SAC 998314
- [ ] Verify all deliverables, source code, and documentation meet contractual quality standards
- [ ] Transition completed engagement to `customer-success-manager` and `handover-specialist`
- [ ] Compile Engagement Closeout Report and conduct client post-delivery review
- [ ] Confirm zero em dashes are present in any delivery documentation
- [ ] Format handoff according to `orchestration/handoff-format.md` standards

## Stop and escalate if
- [ ] Engagement gross margin falls below 35% with significant unfinished scope remaining
- [ ] Client refuses to sign milestone acceptance certificate without legitimate technical grounds
- [ ] Overdue client invoice exceeds 30 days past contractual due date
