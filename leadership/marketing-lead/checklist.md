# Marketing Lead: Checklist

## Before starting
- [ ] Sales target for the quarter received from `sales-lead` (not invented)
- [ ] Conversion rates obtained or marked `[Assumption]`
- [ ] Budget in INR and founder hours per week for marketing known
- [ ] Last 90 days of channel results available (or exports requested)
- [ ] Current positioning and offer list read

## While working
- [ ] Funnel computed backwards from projects to qualified enquiries
- [ ] Enquiries split by channel using last quarter's sources
- [ ] One budget method chosen and the math shown
- [ ] 10 to 20% experiment budget included when any money is allocated
- [ ] 70/20/10 split applied (or 50/30/20 with no evidence)
- [ ] Hours per channel fit within founder hours
- [ ] At most 3 priorities, each with owner, indicator, target, kill criterion, review date
- [ ] Compounding assets preferred over one-off spikes
- [ ] Channel decisions cite measured numbers or say "insufficient data"
- [ ] Small monthly numbers read as direction only
- [ ] No follower or impression count used as a target
- [ ] Claims checked for evidence and client permission
- [ ] Creator collaborations flagged for ASCI disclosure check (verify current guidance)

## Before handoff
- [ ] Plan fits on one page with a "not doing" list
- [ ] Channel briefs include budget, hours and targets
- [ ] KPI definitions agreed with `marketing-analyst`
- [ ] Assumptions, open questions and risks listed
- [ ] Handoff written in `orchestration/handoff-format.md` format with evidence

## Stop and escalate if
- [ ] Any paid spend, trial or ad budget change is proposed
- [ ] Client names, logos or results would appear without written permission
- [ ] The plan needs more founder hours than the owner gave
- [ ] A campaign makes performance promises to prospects
