# Code of conduct

This project welcomes contributors of every background and experience level. We want collaboration to be respectful, constructive and focused on making the framework better.

## Expected behaviour
- Be respectful and assume good faith. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Give specific, evidence-based feedback and accept it the same way.
- Respect privacy. Do not post other people's personal information, client details or credentials.
- Credit sources. Do not present others' work as your own.
- Keep discussion on topic and professional.

## Unacceptable behaviour
- Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks or demeaning comments.
- Publishing private information about others without consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews.
- Submitting content you do not have the right to contribute.

## Scope
This applies in all project spaces, such as issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews, and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and may warn, temporarily restrict or permanently ban participants who breach this code. Report concerns privately to the maintainers through the channel described in `SECURITY.md`. Reports are handled with discretion. The repository owner should add a dedicated conduct contact before the project is promoted (see `docs/release-readiness/`).
