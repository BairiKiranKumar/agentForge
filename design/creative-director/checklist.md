# Creative Director: Checklist

## Before starting
- [ ] Brief states audience, the one action that matters, channels and deadline
- [ ] Brand platform (`brand-strategist`) and identity assets (`brand-designer`) read, or their absence noted
- [ ] Locked assets and locked sections listed
- [ ] Inventory of real material done: owned photos (count usable), founder story, menu or service list, reviews
- [ ] Ten-noun subject list written from the business's own world
- [ ] Budget for shoots, stock or 3D confirmed with Kiran, or assumed zero

## While working
- [ ] Two or three routes, each from a different angle (outcome, identity, proof, place, craft)
- [ ] Each route cites the real input it came from
- [ ] Wireframe test passed: no two routes would produce the same layout
- [ ] Token sketch per route: 4 to 6 named hex values, typefaces and roles, layout idea, two principles
- [ ] Plan-then-attack check run; defaults found and replacements recorded
- [ ] No banned patterns: gradient blobs, pill badges everywhere, identical three-card rows, emoji icons
- [ ] Exactly one signature moment per page or series, with a static fallback
- [ ] Moodboard references each have a source and a "borrow / do not copy" note
- [ ] Regional script use names a real font with script support and licence status flagged
- [ ] Festival or cultural elements used as narrative timing, not decoration
- [ ] Hero media fits a mid-range Android on 4G, or a fallback is defined

## Before handoff
- [ ] Recommendation table: audience fit, material fit, build cost, risk
- [ ] Art direction sheet complete in every field
- [ ] Photo brief has 8 to 15 frames mapped to sections, or a type-led or illustration-led alternative
- [ ] Banned treatments list included for `ui-designer`, `illustrator`, `canva-specialist`
- [ ] No invented claims, stats or testimonials anywhere in the routes
- [ ] Assumptions, open questions and risks listed
- [ ] Handoff written in `orchestration/handoff-format.md` format with evidence

## Stop and escalate if
- [ ] A route needs spend (photographer, stock, 3D artist) without approval
- [ ] The client wants to imitate a named competitor's look
- [ ] The concept relies on AI-generated people shown as real customers or staff
- [ ] Religious or community imagery is involved without client sign-off

## Design Intelligence upgrade
- [ ] Tier (S, M or L) recorded with the reason; exploration skipped only for Tier S
- [ ] Research brief and motion shortlist read; entry ids cited in the routes
- [ ] Difference test written for every pair of routes
- [ ] Default replaced on each axis recorded, using the anti-slop signal list
- [ ] Signature moment prototype reviewed at 375 and 1440 px before full build (high-visibility work)
- [ ] `direction-approval.md` written with rubric profile and reviewer before major implementation
- [ ] Rejections of downstream work cite the direction element, the evidence and the change needed
