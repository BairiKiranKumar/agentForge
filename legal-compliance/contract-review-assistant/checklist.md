# Contract Review Assistant: Checklist

## Before starting
- [ ] Full document set received, including schedules and referenced terms
- [ ] the owner's side and agreement type confirmed
- [ ] Fee, timeline and deliverables known
- [ ] Deadline recorded and whether a lawyer can review in time
- [ ] Language of the document noted; translation is not a legal review

## While working
- [ ] Whole document read once before flagging
- [ ] Each material clause rated against the studio playbook
- [ ] YELLOW and RED items quote text with section numbers
- [ ] Liability cap base quoted verbatim; carve-outs listed
- [ ] IP: present-tense assignment, transfer on full payment, pre-existing IP excluded, licence back checked
- [ ] Copyright assignment duration and territory stated (Section 19 defaults flagged if missing)
- [ ] Moral rights waivers flagged for the lawyer
- [ ] AI-generated deliverables vs "original work" promises checked
- [ ] Non-compete or restraint clauses flagged
- [ ] Payment tied to objective milestones, not satisfaction
- [ ] Termination pays for work done
- [ ] Governing law and dispute venue in India checked
- [ ] All 17 missing clause items checked
- [ ] Dates and obligations extracted
- [ ] Citations tagged by source
- [ ] Wording suggestions use the smallest edit

## Before handoff
- [ ] "Not legal advice" header and one-line bottom line present
- [ ] No "enforceable", "legal" or "safe to sign" conclusions
- [ ] Lawyer questions written for every RED item
- [ ] Assumptions, open questions and risks listed
- [ ] Handoff written in `orchestration/handoff-format.md` format with evidence

## Stop and escalate if
- [ ] Any RED item and the owner wants to sign before lawyer review
- [ ] Counterparty is foreign, a government body, or the fee exceeds Rs. 1 lakh on their paper
- [ ] Asked to sign, e-sign or send changes
- [ ] Asked to give a legal opinion or stamp duty answer
