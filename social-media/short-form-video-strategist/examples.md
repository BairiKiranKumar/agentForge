## Example 1: Before/after reveal for Reels and Shorts
**Request:** "Make a short from the school website speed work. I have screen recordings of the old and new site on a throttled phone."

**Key decisions:**
- Reveal structure, 20 seconds; the side-by-side load is the whole story.
- Numbers from Kiran's measurement (3.1 MB to 900 KB, 2026-10-12). School unnamed per permission.
- No music; a single tick sound when the new site finishes loading.

**Output (excerpt):**
```
Video ID: SF-2026-10-29-1   Platforms: Reels, Shorts   Channel: @kiranbuildswithai   Language: English
Structure: Reveal   Target length: 20 s   Series: One Fix Friday
Hooks: A) visual: two phones side by side, both blank / spoken: none / text: "Same school site. Same 4G."
       B) visual: old site still loading / text: "Parents waited 9 seconds for this"
       C) visual: new site loaded instantly / text: "3.1 MB vs 900 KB"
Chosen: A, because the tension builds while both load and the reveal pays it off
| Time | Visual | Spoken | On-screen text | Edit note |
| 0-2 | both phones blank | none | "Same school site. Same 4G." | static, no zoom |
| 2-6 | new site appears on the right | none | "After" | tick sound on full load |
| 6-12 | old site still loading | none | "Before: 3.1 MB" | slow punch-in on the spinner |
| 12-17 | old site finally loads | none | "After: 900 KB" | cut back to both |
| 17-20 | enquiry button tapped on new site | none | "4 changes. Part 1 tomorrow" | loop to first frame |
Edit notes: text in upper-middle third; captions bold with dark box; export 1080x1920, 30 fps
Music: none   Consent: no people shown
```

## Example 2: Telugu vlog Short series
**Request:** "My Telugu vlog Shorts are random. Give me a series."

**Key decisions:**
- Analytics show the three best Shorts were all Nizamabad food spots with the price shown early.
- Series: "₹100 lo Nizamabad" (what you get for ₹100), 30 to 40 seconds, weekly. Prices only as actually paid on camera.
- Telugu on-screen text reviewed by `localization-specialist`; Noto Sans Telugu.

**Output (excerpt):**
| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Series name | ₹100 lo Nizamabad |
| Promise | One place, what ₹100 actually gets you, honest verdict |
| Fixed opening | ₹100 note on the counter, text "₹100 lo ఏమొస్తుంది?" |
| Structure | 0-2 note and place name, 2-20 order and first bite, 20-32 verdict, 32-36 location text |
| Length | 30 to 40 s |
| Rules | ask shop owner before filming staff; blur other customers' faces |

## Example 3: Boundary: film song on a gaming Short
**Request:** "Put the new Telugu film song behind my BGMI clutch clip, it's trending."

**Response (excerpt):** "I'd avoid it. A film song is almost certainly claimed, and on YouTube a Short over one minute with an active claim is blocked (YouTube Help, checked 2026-10-20); under a minute it can still be claimed or muted. Your clip is 48 seconds, so keep it under a minute and use a track from the YouTube Audio Library, or no music with the game audio and your reaction, which is what your best clutch Short used. I've written the beat sheet both ways."
