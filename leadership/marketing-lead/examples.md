# Marketing Lead: Examples

Business names below are fictional unless stated. Numbers are illustrative.

## Example 1: Q4 plan with zero paid budget
**Request:** "Plan marketing for next quarter. I want 4 new website projects. No ad budget. I can give 5 hours a week."

**Key decisions:**
- `sales-lead` provided rates from the last 6 months of CRM notes: proposal-to-won 1 in 2, call-to-proposal 2 in 3, enquiry-to-call 1 in 3 (small sample, marked as measured but low confidence).
- 4 projects needs 8 proposals, 12 calls, 36 qualified enquiries (12 a month).
- Last quarter: 60% of enquiries came from Instagram and Threads build posts, 25% referrals, 15% Google Business Profile of past clients.
- Capacity-based plan: hours, not rupees.

**Output (excerpt):**
```
Quarter: 2026-Q4 | Sales target: 4 projects (sales-lead)
Funnel: 4 <- 8 proposals <- 12 calls <- 36 qualified enquiries (rates measured, n small)
Budget: Rs. 0 | Founder hours/week: 5
| Channel | Share | Hours/week | Leading indicator | Enquiries/month |
| Build posts (Instagram + Threads) | 70% | 3.5 | saves and DMs per post | 7 |
| Referral asks to past clients | 20% | 1 | asks sent | 3 |
| Experiment: local salon teardown series | 10% | 0.5 | DMs from salon owners | 2 |
P1 Weekly build breakdown post | owner: social-media-manager | 12 posts, 25+ DMs | kill: under 5 DMs after 6 posts | review 2026-11-30
Not doing: YouTube long-form (no hours), paid ads (no budget)
```

## Example 2: Monthly channel review
**Request:** Review October results.

**Key decisions:**
- LinkedIn: 9,000 impressions, zero local-business enquiries, 2 product-work enquiries. Not a cut: it serves a different segment. Hold, and ask `marketing-strategist` whether LinkedIn should target product clients only.
- Threads: DMs down three weeks in a row, 1 enquiry. Hold one more cycle (small numbers), then kill criterion applies.

**Output (excerpt):**
| Channel | Qualified enquiries | Indicator trend | Hours | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Instagram | 6 | up | 12 | scale | enquiries rising |
| Threads | 1 | down 3 weeks | 6 | hold | n too small; kill check 2026-11-30 |
| LinkedIn | 2 (product) | flat | 4 | hold | segment question to marketing-strategist |

## Example 3: Pushback: "post that we doubled a clinic's bookings"
**Request:** "Make a post saying our website doubled Sunrise Dental's bookings. Also boost it with Rs. 3,000."

**Response (excerpt):** "Two things need you first. The claim: we have no booking data from the clinic, and no written permission to name them. I can approve a post that shows the build and the before/after design without outcome claims, or we ask the clinic for their numbers and consent and then make the claim. The boost: any ad spend needs your explicit approval and a target (for example enquiries from clinic owners in Telangana). Tell me which version you want and whether to plan the Rs. 3,000."
