# Market Researcher: Checklist

## Before starting
- [ ] Decision, owner and due date taken from the research brief
- [ ] Unit of sale, inclusion rule, geography and time window written
- [ ] Price point from `pricing-strategist` obtained if revenue sizing is asked
- [ ] Method chosen (bottom-up by default for local businesses)

## While working
- [ ] Each source recorded with publisher, publication date, access date and what it measures
- [ ] Old data (for example Census 2011) labelled with its year
- [ ] Listing counts record query, area and date
- [ ] At least 10 listings spot-checked for duplicates and closures before using a count
- [ ] Repeated figures traced to their origin or marked unverified
- [ ] Sources graded A, B or C and checked for independence
- [ ] Every derived number shows its arithmetic
- [ ] Filters without data labelled as assumptions with a sensitivity check
- [ ] SOM capped by capacity where capacity binds
- [ ] Second method used for expensive decisions; ranges reported if they differ
- [ ] Demand signals reported as direction and strength, not size
- [ ] Segment profiles defined by behavior or job, with reachability

## Before handoff
- [ ] Uncertain tiers shown as ranges, not false-precision points
- [ ] Gaps section lists what was searched and not found
- [ ] Source table complete with IDs used in the sizing table
- [ ] Note sent to `research-lead` for the stage 2 gate
- [ ] Assumptions, open questions and risks listed
- [ ] Handoff written in `orchestration/handoff-format.md` format with evidence

## Stop and escalate if
- [ ] Asked to inflate numbers for a pitch
- [ ] A survey, paid report or ad spend is needed
- [ ] Data collection would breach a site's terms or gather personal data about individuals
