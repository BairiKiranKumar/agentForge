# Negotiation Preparation Specialist: Checklist

## Before starting
- [ ] Client's ask quoted exactly, with the proposal version it responds to
- [ ] Deal plan read: approver, champion, competing options, deadline
- [ ] Rate card version, cost floor and discount policy obtained from `pricing-strategist`
- [ ] Kiran's open pipeline and free capacity known
- [ ] Kiran's stated non-negotiables collected

## While working
- [ ] Every issue in the ask listed (price, scope, timeline, milestones, revisions, support, ownership, start date)
- [ ] Each issue ranked for Kiran and for the client, with evidence for the client side
- [ ] Each position mapped to an interest, labelled stated or `[Inference]`
- [ ] Both BATNAs rated with evidence
- [ ] Opening equals the rate card total; no padding
- [ ] Walk-away at or above the floor, or gap flagged for Kiran
- [ ] ZOPA estimated; "none at current scope" stated when true
- [ ] Concessions ranked by cost to Kiran and value to client
- [ ] Every concession has a condition and an approver
- [ ] No more than three concessions planned; sizes decrease
- [ ] Never-concede list written
- [ ] Two or three options equally acceptable to Kiran
- [ ] Scripts written for the three most likely client moves, with "avoid" lines
- [ ] Competing quote handled by asking for its scope, not by matching

## Before handoff
- [ ] Approvals needed listed (Kiran, `sales-lead`, `contract-review-assistant`)
- [ ] Nothing in the sheet presented as agreed
- [ ] Assumptions, open questions and risks listed
- [ ] Handoff written in `orchestration/handoff-format.md` format with evidence
- [ ] Debrief scheduled for after the outcome

## Stop and escalate if
- [ ] The ask involves cash without a bill, split invoices or anything off record
- [ ] The ask changes liability, IP, penalty or long payment terms
- [ ] Asked to invent a deadline or competing offer to pressure the client
- [ ] Asked to agree or send terms on Kiran's behalf
