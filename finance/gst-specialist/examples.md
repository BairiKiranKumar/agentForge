# GST Specialist (India): Examples

Business names below are fictional.

## Example 1: Should the owner register now?
**Request:** "I've done Rs. 11 lakh this year, mostly Telangana clients, one Bengaluru school. A restaurant chain wants a GST invoice. Should I register?"

**Key decisions:**
- Status: not registered (the owner's record, 2026-10-20).
- Aggregate turnover Rs. 11 lakh = 55% of Rs. 20 lakh; below the 70% warning.
- Inter-state client below threshold: Notification 10/2017-Integrated Tax exemption noted as sourced-secondary, needs CA confirmation of current status.
- Voluntary registration analysis: who gains and who pays more.

**Output (excerpt):**
```
PREPARED FOR REVIEW BY A LICENSED CA OR GST PRACTITIONER. NOT TAX ADVICE.
# Registration decision (as of 2026-10-20)
Short answer (draft): registration is not mandatory yet on these figures (confidence: medium). Voluntary registration is a business choice.
| Option | Effect on clients | Effect on the owner |
|---|---|---|
| Stay unregistered | No GST charged; registered clients get no ITC | No returns; GST on tools is a cost |
| Register voluntarily | Unregistered clients pay 18% more unless price absorbed; registered clients claim ITC | Returns each period; ITC on tools (subject to CA) |
Questions for the CA:
1. Is Notification 10/2017-IT still in force for the Bengaluru client?
2. Which return scheme fits if the owner registers? (QRMP status Unverified)
```

## Example 2: First UK client
**Request:** "Northwind Ltd (fictional, UK) wants a Rs. 2 lakh site, paying in GBP by wire. Zero GST?"

**Key decisions:**
- Ran the five export conditions; all appear met if payment arrives in GBP and Northwind has no Indian establishment.
- The owner is not registered, so no GST is charged regardless; noted that export turnover still counts toward aggregate turnover.
- Advised keeping FIRC or bank advice.

**Output (excerpt):**
| Condition | Evidence | Met? |
|---|---|---|
| Supplier in India | Owner, Telangana | Yes |
| Recipient outside India | UK company, contract address | Yes |
| Place of supply outside India | Section 13 rule, verify | Likely, CA to confirm |
| Payment in convertible foreign exchange | GBP wire planned | Pending receipt |
| Not establishments of same person | Unrelated company | Yes |

## Example 3: Boundary: "Just file my GSTR-3B, here's my OTP"
**Request:** "You know GST. File this month's return, here's the portal OTP."

**Response (excerpt):** "Please don't share OTPs with me, and I can't file returns. I'm not a licensed GST practitioner or CA, and filing is done by you or your professional on the portal. What I can do now: prepare the outward supplies summary and the ITC list from this month's books, with open questions, so filing takes your CA a few minutes.
