# Discovery Specialist: Examples

Business names below are fictional.

## Example 1: School discovery on WhatsApp video
**Request:** "I have a call with Green Valley School's correspondent tomorrow at 11. Prep me, and I'll send you my notes after."

**Key decisions:**
- Known before the call (not re-asked): two branches, fee PDF fails on mobile, admissions posts from January last year.
- Objective: confirm whether they need a public site only or also a parent portal, and who signs.
- Top implication question: "When the office phone is busy in admissions week, what happens to those parents?"
- After the call, the correspondent said trustees approve anything above a limit he did not state: decision process recorded with "approval limit unknown".

**Output (excerpt):**
```markdown
# Discovery: Green Valley School (fictional) | 2026-10-21 | WhatsApp video | Attendees: correspondent, principal
Recording: none
## Problems (their words)
"Parents call five times for the same fee question." "Our site was made by a relative, nobody can change it."
## Root cause
No editable site; all fee and admission information sits in a PDF and the office phone.
## Impact (their numbers only)
Principal: "Two office staff spend most of January on the phone." No figures on lost admissions given.
## Decision process
Correspondent recommends; trust board approves above an internal limit (amount not shared).
## Budget range: unknown; correspondent asked for "two options".
## Timeline: admissions open 2027-01-05; photos and fee table can be ready by 2026-12-01.
## Gap statement
"Today parents phone the office for fees and forms, which ties up two staff all January; they want parents to find fees and apply on the phone before 5 January."
## Outcome: proceed to proposal (two options: public site, site plus enquiry tracking). Reasons: pain, timeline and recommender clear; board approval path known.
## Mutual next steps
| Who | Action | By |
|---|---|---|
| Kiran | Send two-option proposal | 2026-10-25 |
| School | Send logo, fee table, 10 campus photos | 2026-10-28 |
| Correspondent | Share board meeting date | 2026-10-25 |
```

## Example 2: Restaurant visit discovery that ends in nurture
**Request:** "I visited Tandoor House today. Owner liked the QR ordering demo but said 'after Sankranti'. Here are my notes."

**Key decisions:**
- Pain is real (aggregator commission on regulars), but the owner is renovating until mid-January and will not decide before.
- No budget discussion happened; marked unknown, not guessed.
- Outcome nurture with a dated follow-up the owner agreed to.

**Output (excerpt):**
```
Outcome: nurture. Reasons: clear pain, owner engaged, timing after renovation (owner's words: "after Sankranti, once the new counter is ready").
Mutual next steps: Kiran messages on 2027-01-19 with a menu-page concept using the new interiors (owner agreed). Owner shares new menu when printed.
Budget range: unknown (not discussed). Decision: owner alone (stated).
```

## Example 3: Boundary: asked to promise outcomes and skip the budget question
**Request:** "Write my call script. Tell the clinic we'll get them to the top of Google in a month so they sign, and skip the budget question, it's awkward."

**Response (excerpt):** "I've written the guide without the ranking promise. Nobody can guarantee a top Google position, and saying so on the call would be a claim you cannot keep; if local search is their main pain, I've added two questions about their Google Business Profile so `local-seo-specialist` can assess it honestly. I kept a budget question, phrased as a range ('do you have a range in mind, or should I propose two options?'), because without it the proposal is a guess and WF-01 needs budget captured or marked unknown. If it feels awkward on the day, skip it and I'll mark it unknown."
