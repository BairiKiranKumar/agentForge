# People Operations Specialist Checklist

## Before starting
- [ ] Verify candidate offer letter, agreed CTC in INR, and target joining date from `recruiting-specialist`
- [ ] Confirm employee identity documents: PAN card, masked Aadhaar card, and bank account details
- [ ] Check employee tax regime selection (Old vs New Tax Regime under Income-tax Act)
- [ ] Verify contractor vs full-time employee legal classification
- [ ] Ensure consent notice is recorded for personal data processing under DPDP Act 2023

## While working
- [ ] Structure CTC breakdown sheet ensuring Basic salary complies with minimum wage rules
- [ ] Calculate statutory employer and employee contributions (EPF, ESI, Professional Tax)
- [ ] Prepare standard employment agreement or independent contractor agreement
- [ ] Log employee in central HR register and assign unique employee identification number
- [ ] Compile monthly attendance records, approved paid leaves, and unpaid leaves of absence
- [ ] Calculate monthly gross earnings, tax deductions under Section 192, and net take-home pay
- [ ] Maintain POSH compliance calendar: track annual Internal Committee meetings and filings
- [ ] Audit digital employee records to ensure zero unmasked Aadhaar numbers are stored

## Before handoff
- [ ] Review monthly payroll register for 100% mathematical accuracy across all rows
- [ ] Submit finalized payroll register to `budgeting-specialist` and `operations-lead` by the 25th
- [ ] Transmit signed contract confirmation to `employee-onboarding-specialist`
- [ ] Archive all executed agreements, tax declarations, and clearance forms in secure repository
- [ ] Confirm zero em dashes are present in any HR documentation
- [ ] Format handoff according to `orchestration/handoff-format.md` standards

## Stop and escalate if
- [ ] Formal workplace harassment (POSH) complaint received (mandatory 24-hour IC escalation)
- [ ] Discrepancy in identity documentation suggests potential identity fraud
- [ ] Proposed employment terms violate statutory minimum wage, PF, or state labor regulations
