# Conversion Optimization Specialist: Checklist

## Before starting
- [ ] Goal action defined and its tracking location confirmed (form submit, WhatsApp click, call tap, booking)
- [ ] 28 days of sessions by device and source obtained
- [ ] Monthly sessions on the page checked against the 1,000 session threshold
- [ ] Conversion tracking verified for double counting (form and thank-you page)
- [ ] Current offer and price recorded with an as-of date
- [ ] Staging or live link to the page for a mobile check

## Audit
- [ ] Value proposition understood within 5 seconds on a 360 px wide Android screen
- [ ] Headline states an outcome or specific offer, and matches the traffic source
- [ ] One primary call to action per screen, label states what happens next
- [ ] Call to action visible above the fold on mobile
- [ ] Trust signals present: real photos, named staff, sourced reviews, registered address and phone
- [ ] Price or price range visible with an as-of date
- [ ] Top three objections (price, time, trust) answered near the action
- [ ] Form fields counted; each extra field justified

## Mobile and Indian context
- [ ] Phone input shows the numeric keyboard and accepts 10 digits with or without +91
- [ ] WhatsApp link opens a prefilled chat on a common Android browser
- [ ] Page load checked on a mid-range Android device on a throttled network
- [ ] Regional language copy considered for the audience, with native review planned
- [ ] Pop-ups do not cover the call to action on mobile

## Tests
- [ ] Hypothesis has a source of evidence; otherwise it is labelled idea
- [ ] Priority score calculated (impact times confidence divided by effort)
- [ ] Primary metric, guardrails and decision rule written before launch
- [ ] Sample size calculated with a stated baseline and minimum detectable effect
- [ ] Test runs for at least two full weeks and stops only at the calculated sample
- [ ] Variant checked for layout and accessibility (contrast, tap target size, labels) before launch
- [ ] Test log entry created with dates

## Reporting and handoff
- [ ] Result reported with sample, difference, confidence level and guardrails
- [ ] Learning recorded even when the variant loses
- [ ] Winning change handed to the developer with expected lift labelled as an estimate

## Stop and escalate if
- [ ] Monthly sessions are below 1,000 and the client wants a formal A/B test
- [ ] The variant changes the price, offer or a legal disclosure
- [ ] The test shows a lead quality drop while submits rise
- [ ] Session recordings would capture unmasked personal data
