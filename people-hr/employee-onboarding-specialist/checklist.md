# Employee Onboarding Specialist Checklist

## Before starting
- [ ] Confirm signed contract, official start date, and role details from `people-operations-specialist`
- [ ] Obtain technical requirements and software license list from department lead
- [ ] Confirm designated onboarding buddy assignment and verify their bandwidth
- [ ] Verify that corporate email, Slack, and Google Workspace accounts are pre-created
- [ ] Prepare customized 30-day onboarding roadmap with clear weekly milestones

## While working
- [ ] Send welcome email packet to new hire 3 days before start date with Day 1 schedule
- [ ] Host Day 1 welcome call at 10:00 IST and assist with MFA security setup
- [ ] Facilitate initial introduction sync between new hire and onboarding buddy
- [ ] Post welcome announcement in company Slack channel `#general`
- [ ] Check in with new hire at end of Day 1 to ensure all core tools are accessible
- [ ] Conduct Week 1 Friday retrospective to review initial impressions and unblock blockers
- [ ] Track progress against 30-day milestone deliverables with manager and buddy
- [ ] Administer Day 30 onboarding experience survey

## Before handoff
- [ ] Host formal 30-day onboarding review meeting with new hire and department lead
- [ ] Document feedback, celebrate first major wins, and identify ongoing development goals
- [ ] Verify all required administrative compliance training has been completed
- [ ] Confirm zero em dashes are present in any onboarding documentation
- [ ] Transition employee records to standard active monitoring and archive onboarding files
- [ ] Format handoff according to `orchestration/handoff-format.md` standards

## Stop and escalate if
- [ ] New hire cannot access core software systems by 12:00 IST on Day 1
- [ ] New hire reports severe interpersonal friction, harassment, or unprofessional conduct
- [ ] Clear performance red flags emerge within the first 14 days indicating fundamental skill gap
