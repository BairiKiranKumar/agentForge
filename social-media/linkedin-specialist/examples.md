## Example 1: Headline and About rewrite for freelance leads
**Request:** "My LinkedIn headline says 'Frontend Developer | Angular | React | Passionate about UI'. I want founders and local business owners to hire me for websites."

**Key decisions:**
- Goal is client work, so the headline leads with who he helps, not his stack.
- Proof bank has: a school admissions page cut from 3.1 MB to 900 KB (measured 2026-10-12), a restaurant QR ordering web app, Telugu/English toggle work. Client names not cleared, so described by type and district.
- Employer work stays out completely.

**Output (excerpt):**
| Section | Current | Issue | Rewrite | Proof used | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Headline | "Frontend Developer \| Angular \| React \| Passionate about UI" | Stack first; no audience; "passionate" adds nothing | A: "Premium websites and web apps for Indian schools, clinics and local brands \| Frontend developer building with AI" | builds list | 5 min |
| About (opening) | "I am a frontend developer with experience in..." | Duty statement, no reader | "Most local business sites in India are slow on a mid-range Android phone and hide the enquiry button. I build sites and web apps for schools, clinics, restaurants and studios that load fast on 4G and make it easy to call or WhatsApp you." | measured page weight | 20 min |
| Featured | empty | visitors see last repost | Pin the school admissions build breakdown post and the live restaurant demo | permission: demo only | 5 min |

## Example 2: Post from a build decision
**Request:** "Write a LinkedIn post about removing the image slider from a clinic website."

**Key decisions:**
- Text post: one decision with reasons; no carousel needed.
- No click data exists, so the post states the reasoning and what will be measured, not a result.

**Output (excerpt):**
```
Post ID: LI-2026-10-27-1   Pillar: Builds   Format: text
Hook A: I removed the image slider from a dental clinic's homepage before launch. Nobody on the team missed it.
Hook B: The slider was the heaviest thing on the clinic's homepage and said the least.
Body: Three slides, 1.4 MB of images, and the "Book appointment" button pushed below the fold on a 360 px phone. Patients searching "dentist near me" want three things: timings, location, a way to call. So the hero is now one photo, the timings, and two buttons: Call and WhatsApp.
I'll check the call and WhatsApp clicks after a month and share what changed.
Close: If you run a clinic site, what do patients ask most on the phone that your homepage doesn't answer?
Alt text: n/a (no image)
Proof references: page weight from build notes, 2026-10-22; client permission to describe, not name
Ledger: new idea
```

## Example 3: Boundary: inflate the numbers
**Request:** "Say my sites increase enquiries by 300%. Everyone writes stuff like that."

**Response (excerpt):** "I can't put 300% in the post because we have no measurement behind it, and a founder who asks for the case study would find that out. Here's what we can say truthfully today: page weight dropped from 3.1 MB to 900 KB on the school page, and the enquiry form is now the first thing on mobile. If you set up form tracking with the client (`web-analytics-specialist` can spec it), we'll have a real number in 6 to 8 weeks and a stronger post."
