# India Business Reference (verify before use)

Shared starting points for finance, legal-compliance, marketing and sales agents. **This is not advice and not a substitute for a licensed CA, GST practitioner or lawyer.** Every item has an as-of date and a source status. Agents must re-check current primary sources (CBIC, GST portal, Income Tax Department, gazette notifications, official platform pages) before relying on any number, and must state the as-of date in their output.

Legend: **Sourced** = checked against a published secondary source during the build on 2026-10-20 (link given). **Unverified** = from general knowledge, not checked during the build.
