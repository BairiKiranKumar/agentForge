# LinkedIn Specialist: Checklist

## Before starting
- [ ] Goal and target reader written (client enquiries, authority, hiring); job search and freelancing not mixed without a decision
- [ ] Profile text supplied by the owner (pasted or PDF export); nothing scraped
- [ ] Proof bank listed with dates, measurements and permission status
- [ ] Employer and NDA work identified so it stays out of every draft
- [ ] Services and availability to promote confirmed; no prices unless supplied
- [ ] Reuse ledger opened

## While working
- [ ] Profile audit follows the order: Featured, headline, About fold, experience, skills, recommendations, banner
- [ ] Headline options front-load audience or outcome in the first 60 characters
- [ ] About opening completes two sentences before the fold
- [ ] Experience bullets are outcomes, not duties
- [ ] Each post's format chosen from the material, with the carousel test applied
- [ ] Each post has a complete sentence inside the first 140 characters
- [ ] Two hook options per post
- [ ] One idea per post; body in 2 to 4 line blocks
- [ ] Links moved to the first comment and flagged as a working hypothesis, not a rule
- [ ] No pseudo-bold, no engagement-bait close, no em dashes, 0 to 3 hashtags
- [ ] Document scripts: slide headlines under 12 words, every slide stands alone

## Before handoff
- [ ] Every number has a proof reference with a date
- [ ] Alt text written for images; edited captions required for videos
- [ ] Client mentions checked against permission records
- [ ] Limits stated with as-of date or [Unverified] label
- [ ] Ledger updated with each idea and planned date
- [ ] Drafts reviewed by `editor`; headline and About by `copywriter`
- [ ] Assumptions, open questions and risks listed
- [ ] Handoff written in `orchestration/handoff-format.md` format with evidence

## Stop and escalate if
- [ ] A draft needs a client result or name with no permission record
- [ ] Content touches employer-confidential or client-NDA work
- [ ] Someone asks for fake recommendations, testimonials or inflated numbers
- [ ] Someone asks for automated connection requests, likes or comments
- [ ] You are asked to log in to the account or edit the profile directly
