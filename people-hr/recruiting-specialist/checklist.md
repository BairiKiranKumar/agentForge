# Recruiting Specialist Checklist

## Before starting
- [ ] Verify approved hiring requisition with role title, employment type, and target start date
- [ ] Confirm approved annual CTC or contractor hourly/monthly budget in INR with `budgeting-specialist`
- [ ] Define core role competencies and required technical skills with hiring manager
- [ ] Review current market compensation benchmarks for the role in Indian tech hubs
- [ ] Check if candidate consent mechanisms align with DPDP Act 2023 requirements

## While working
- [ ] Draft public-facing job posting including transparent compensation range and remote terms
- [ ] Design structured interview kit with behaviorally anchored rating scales (BARS)
- [ ] Create synthetic, non-exploitative practical work-sample prompt (capped at 2-3 hours)
- [ ] Source candidates across diverse channels (LinkedIn, GitHub, tech communities)
- [ ] Screen inbound resumes against objective competency criteria
- [ ] Conduct 30-minute structured initial screening calls focusing on skills and logistics
- [ ] Brief technical interviewers on structured question techniques and bias prevention
- [ ] Collect independent written scorecards from all interviewers prior to group discussion
- [ ] Check notice period duration (30, 60, or 90 days) and verify counter-offer risk

## Before handoff
- [ ] Compile complete Candidate Evaluation Dossier with all completed scorecards
- [ ] Formulate offer recommendation detailing proposed CTC structure in INR
- [ ] Obtain hiring sign-off from hiring manager and `operations-lead`
- [ ] Confirm zero em dashes are present in any recruitment documentation
- [ ] Hand over candidate contact details and resume to `people-operations-specialist`
- [ ] Format handoff according to `orchestration/handoff-format.md` standards

## Stop and escalate if
- [ ] Interviewers request candidate rejection based on protected characteristics or subjective intuition
- [ ] Top candidate demands compensation exceeding the maximum approved budget band by >15%
- [ ] Critical hiring deadline cannot be met due to candidate 90-day notice period constraint
