# Kickoff Facilitator: Examples

Business names below are fictional unless stated.

## Example 1: Facilitating kickoff for boutique fashion brand
**Request:** "Run the project kickoff for Kavitha Sarees website redesign."

**Key decisions:**
- Designed a 45-minute agenda focusing on brand aesthetic, photography deadlines, and Diwali launch timeline.
- Established roles: Smt. Kavitha (Brand Owner) is sole design approver; her store manager handles product photo uploads.
- Agreed on working rhythms: weekly design reviews every Wednesday at 15:00 IST; day-to-day coordination via dedicated WhatsApp group.
- Emphasized the 48-hour review turnaround rule to hit the Diwali festival shopping launch date.

**Output (excerpt):**
```markdown
# Kickoff Summary: Kavitha Sarees Redesign
- Date: 2026-10-21 (15:00 - 15:45 IST) | Channel: Google Meet
- Attendees: The owner (Studio Lead), Smt. Kavitha (Owner), Mr. Rajesh (Store Manager)

## Agreed Decisions & Protocols
- Core Goal: Launch modern mobile-first e-commerce catalog before Diwali festival shopping surge.
- Decision Maker: Smt. Kavitha has sole sign-off authority for visual designs and homepage layout.
- Review Turnaround: Client committed to 48-hour review window for design concepts.
- Communication: WhatsApp group 'Kavitha Sarees x Studio' for rapid coordination.

## Week 1 Action Items
1. Smt. Kavitha: Share brand logo vector files and raw festive saree photo drive by Oct 24.
2. The owner: Deliver 2 visual homepage concepts by Oct 28.
```

## Example 2: Handling scope expansion during kickoff call
**Request:** "During the clinic website kickoff, the client brings up wanting a full patient mobile app and multilingual voice assistant."

**Key decisions:**
- Intervened diplomatically without disrupting meeting rapport.
- Acknowledged the idea: validated that a voice assistant would be powerful for elderly patients.
- Grounded discussion in the agreed baseline: pointed out that the current engagement is focused on launching the core web appointment booking platform in 4 weeks.
- Action taken: recorded the voice assistant under "Future Roadmap Ideas" and agreed to review in Phase 2 after web launch.

**Output (excerpt):**
```markdown
### Facilitator Intervention Log: Scope Alignment
- Client Remark: Dr. Rao inquired about adding a Telugu voice assistant for appointment booking during Slide 4.
- Facilitation Action: "That is an innovative idea for patient accessibility, Dr. Rao. To ensure we hit our target launch date of Nov 30 for the online booking portal, our current phase focuses strictly on the web booking flow. Let us document the Telugu voice assistant under our Phase 2 Innovation Roadmap, and we will prepare a dedicated proposal for it once the website is successfully live."
- Outcome: Dr. Rao agreed; meeting remained on schedule.
```

## Example 3: Boundary: client decision-maker sends non-authorized proxy
**Request:** "At the scheduled kickoff for a school portal, the school Chairman is absent and sends a junior student intern who says: 'The Chairman is busy, just tell me what to do and I'll pass it along.'"

**Response (excerpt):** "Welcome, and thank you for joining us today. However, the purpose of our project kickoff is to establish binding agreements on project scope, design approval authority, and delivery milestone dates. A proxy cannot make binding decisions on school branding, legal data consent, or sign-off authority. To ensure we do not waste your time or risk misaligned expectations, I will pause today's formal kickoff. I will coordinate with the Chairman's office via `onboarding-manager` to reschedule our 45-minute session to a slot when the Chairman can attend. In the meantime, I will send our orientation packet and asset checklist so you can begin gathering the school photos."
