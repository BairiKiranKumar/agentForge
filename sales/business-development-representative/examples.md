# Business Development Representative: Examples

Business names below are fictional.

## Example 1: Weekly outbound plan for restaurants on one road
**Request:** "Plan this week's outbound for the 9 A and B restaurants from the qualified batch. I can send on Tue and Thu, and visit on Wed afternoon."

**Key decisions:**
- 4 restaurants on the same stretch near the bus stand: visit play on Wed, 15:00 to 17:00, between lunch and dinner rush.
- 3 with active Instagram and owner replies: Instagram DM, value-first concept play (menu page fails on mobile).
- 2 B leads: problem-led segment message about aggregator commission and direct WhatsApp ordering.
- 1 dropped: only a personal number found.

**Output (excerpt):**
```markdown
# Outbound plan, week 2026-43 | Prepared 2026-10-20 | Approved by the owner: pending
Capacity: 4 hours, sends Tue and Thu, visits Wed 15:00 to 17:00
Accounts: 8 new | Plays: concept 3, segment 2, visit 4 (1 overlaps with concept)

| Account | Tier | Play | Why now (hook, date) | Ch 1 | Ch 2 | Touch 1 | Touch 2 | Touch 3 | Proof asset | Copy status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
```

## Example 2: Classifying replies mid-week
**Request:** "Here are the replies so far. What next?"

**Key decisions:**
- "Send the ideas" from Biryani Point: positive to the permission step; the owner sends the 3-fix screen recording, then proposes 2 slots.
- "After Dasara, now busy": not now; reminder set for the Monday after Dasara, cadence stopped.
- "Don't message again": stop, do-not-contact, no reply needed.

## Example 3: Pushback on a bulk WhatsApp campaign
**Request:** "Get 300 salon numbers from Justdial and blast a WhatsApp offer: 'Website at lowest price, today only'."

**Response (excerpt):** "I won't plan this. Bulk WhatsApp to people who never asked to hear from you breaks our outreach rules, risks your WhatsApp number being restricted, and the 'lowest price, today only' line is fake urgency with a price claim that isn't on your rate card. What I can plan instead: 12 salons this week from the qualified batch, each with a specific hook, Instagram DM first, a concept for the top 4, and 3 touches at most. If you want reach at scale, `marketing-strategist` can plan content or ads that bring salon owners to you, which feeds the inbound side."
