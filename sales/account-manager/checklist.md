# Account Manager: Checklist

## Before starting
- [ ] Client list with purchases, launch dates and active plans
- [ ] Outcome health from `customer-success-manager` collected, with date
- [ ] Payment status summary obtained (no bank details)
- [ ] Support and change request history read
- [ ] the owner's capacity for the next 4 to 8 weeks known

## While working
- [ ] Every account tiered (Key, Active, Dormant, Closed) with a next touch date
- [ ] Each planned touch has useful content, not a bare check-in
- [ ] Commercial health rated with reasons
- [ ] Health gate applied: amber or red accounts get no expansion idea
- [ ] Amber and red accounts routed to `retention-specialist` or `collections-coordinator`
- [ ] Every whitespace item cites evidence (their words, a visible change, review data)
- [ ] Items scored on evidence, value, access and capacity; weak items parked with a promoting signal
- [ ] Each idea checked against original scope and support window with `scope-change-manager`
- [ ] No more than 3 ideas per account, each with why now and who cares
- [ ] No prices in idea briefs
- [ ] Referral ask tied to a recorded positive moment
- [ ] Referral ask is specific and forwardable; no request for others' numbers
- [ ] Single-threaded Key accounts have one second-contact action through the owner

## Before handoff
- [ ] Ideas reviewed by `sales-lead` (WF-09 stage 4)
- [ ] Approved ideas briefed to `proposal-specialist` or `account-executive`
- [ ] Opportunities and referrals logged with `crm-operations-specialist`
- [ ] Account plan updated with date and next review
- [ ] Assumptions, open questions and risks listed
- [ ] Handoff written in `orchestration/handoff-format.md` format with evidence

## Stop and escalate if
- [ ] The account has an open dispute or overdue invoice
- [ ] The client asked not to be contacted commercially
- [ ] An "upsell" is a refinement or an item already paid for
- [ ] Asked to offer a referral reward outside the approved policy
