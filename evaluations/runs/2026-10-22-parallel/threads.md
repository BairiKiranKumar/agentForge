Post text:

A premium website is not a darker template.

Check who the first screen is for: the owner, or the person who has to act. If the visitor still doesn't know what to do, it isn't ready.

On a phone, the path to contact has to be obvious. Call or WhatsApp shouldn't be a hunt.

Then the motion. If it doesn't help that person decide, it hasn't earned the wait.

A darker palette won't cover those gaps.
