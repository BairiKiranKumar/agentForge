# Hook: Aarohi Dental Studio sample Reel

Platform: Instagram Reel, 1080 by 1920.
Account: @kiranbuildswithai.
Length: 30 seconds.
Structure: one design decision, result first. `short-form-video-strategist` did not leave a structure for this brief, so this package picks that shape.
Language: English.
Picture: screen recording of the sample page, plus voice. No face.
Status: draft. Do not publish.

The first 1.5 seconds are the hook. No logo opener. No "Hey guys."

## Three options

A) Seen: The sample page is already open. The first screen names families in Nizamabad. The Call or WhatsApp control is in view. Said: This clinic does not exist. On screen: This clinic does not exist

B) Seen: The page scrolls through services and shows no review stars and no quotes. Said: No reviews. None were measured. On screen: No reviews added

C) Seen: The control reads Call or WhatsApp and shows no digits. Said: Where is the phone number? On screen: No number supplied

Chosen: A because @kiranbuildswithai opens on the page itself, and the first spoken line says the clinic is fictional before the sample can be heard as a client.

## Assumptions

- The recording does not exist yet. Hook A is what the first frames must show once `index.html` exists and matches the brief.
- Hooks B and C are alternates. They are later beats in the chosen cut, not the open.

## Risks

- If the fictional line is trimmed, the page can be mistaken for a real clinic. Keep option A as the open.
