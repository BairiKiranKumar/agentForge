# Instagram Specialist: Checklist

## Before starting
- [ ] Account type and goal confirmed (creator growth, enquiries, footfall)
- [ ] Audience, city or area, and customer languages noted
- [ ] Asset list with consent status per person shown (guardian consent for minors)
- [ ] Offer, prices, hours, WhatsApp number and booking path confirmed by the owner
- [ ] Brand kit or Canva template located
- [ ] Platform facts table re-checked; anything older than 3 months re-verified or labelled

## While working
- [ ] Profile audit covers name field, bio, link, action buttons, highlights, pinned posts
- [ ] Last 12 posts reviewed for saves, sends and format mix
- [ ] Every carousel has a named framework chosen before slide copy
- [ ] Slide 1 works alone; headline 8 words or fewer
- [ ] Cover counts delivered exactly, no filler slides
- [ ] Caption line 1 contains a searchable phrase; one CTA only
- [ ] Hashtags specific and at or under the current cap
- [ ] Each Reel concept has visual, spoken and text hooks in the first second
- [ ] Reel text kept out of bottom and right overlay zones; captions planned
- [ ] Reels with more than 3 spoken sentences sent to `scriptwriter`
- [ ] Festival and seasonal posts planned at least 2 weeks ahead
- [ ] Distribution claims cite a dated official statement or are labelled hypotheses

## Before handoff
- [ ] Alt text written for every image and slide
- [ ] No health, beauty or results guarantees; before/after claims routed for compliance review
- [ ] Telugu or Hindi captions sent to `localization-specialist`
- [ ] Design specs handed to `canva-specialist` with dimensions
- [ ] Assumptions, open questions and risks listed
- [ ] Handoff written in `orchestration/handoff-format.md` format with evidence

## Stop and escalate if
- [ ] Content shows customers, patients or students without recorded consent
- [ ] Asked to buy followers, use follow/unfollow, or join engagement pods
- [ ] Asked to post fake reviews or testimonials
- [ ] Music, images or fonts have unclear rights
- [ ] An offer or price would go out without owner confirmation
