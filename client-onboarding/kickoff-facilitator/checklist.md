# Kickoff Facilitator: Checklist

## Before starting
- [ ] Onboarding readiness sign-off confirmed by `onboarding-manager`
- [ ] Advance payment receipt verified with Kiran
- [ ] Client decision-maker attendance confirmed
- [ ] Signed scope baseline and requirements summary reviewed
- [ ] Kickoff agenda drafted and shared with attendees 24 hours prior

## While working
- [ ] Meeting opened on time with warm, professional welcome
- [ ] Team members and client stakeholders introduced with designated roles
- [ ] Project business objectives and success criteria articulated clearly
- [ ] Scope baseline walked through: what is included and what is explicitly excluded
- [ ] Delivery roadmap and key milestone dates presented
- [ ] Working rhythms agreed: weekly demo schedule and preferred communication channels
- [ ] Client review turnaround SLA (48 hours) agreed and documented
- [ ] Live meeting notes taken capturing all verbal decisions
- [ ] Scope disputes or feature additions tabled diplomatically
- [ ] Meeting concluded cleanly within scheduled 45 minutes

## Before handoff
- [ ] Kickoff Notes and Action Items document compiled
- [ ] Every action item verified with a named owner and calendar due date
- [ ] Summary distributed via email and WhatsApp within 4 hours
- [ ] Handoff completed to `delivery-manager` and `client-communication-specialist`
- [ ] Open questions, assumptions and risks recorded
- [ ] Handoff written in `orchestration/handoff-format.md` format

## Stop and escalate if
- [ ] Key client decision-maker is absent from the kickoff meeting
- [ ] Fundamental disagreement emerges regarding the commercial scope baseline
- [ ] Client refuses to commit to a reasonable review turnaround timeline
- [ ] Client attempts to cancel advance payment terms during the kickoff call
