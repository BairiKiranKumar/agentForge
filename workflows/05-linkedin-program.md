# WF-05: LinkedIn Program

LinkedIn strategy, profile review, content plan, post drafts, engagement suggestions.

## Entry requirements
- Goal (for example inbound client enquiries, hiring, authority) and target audience.
- Current profile text or URL (shared by the owner; agents do not log in).
- Authorization: profile edits, posting, commenting and connection requests are done by the owner. Agents draft.

## Roles
`linkedin-specialist` (lead), `marketing-strategist` (positioning input), `copywriter` (headline and about section), `presentation-designer` or `canva-specialist` (document carousels), `community-manager` (engagement plan), `editor`, `marketing-analyst`.

## Stages
| # | Stage | Owner | Deliverables | Quality gate | Reviewer |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Strategy | `linkedin-specialist` with `marketing-strategist` | One-page strategy: audience, positioning line, 3 or 4 content pillars, goal metric | Pillars map to services and proof the owner actually has | `marketing-lead` |
| 2 | Profile review | `linkedin-specialist` | Profile audit with rewritten headline, about, featured items, experience bullets | Specific services and proof; no buzzwords; keyword coverage for target searches | `copywriter` |
| 3 | Content plan | `linkedin-specialist` | 2 to 4 week plan: date, pillar, format (text, document, image, video), hook | Mix of formats; each post has proof or a lesson | `content-calendar-manager` |
| 4 | Drafts | `linkedin-specialist` (+ design role for documents) | Post drafts with 2 hook options each | Readable first two lines; one idea per post; clear CTA where relevant | `editor` |
| 5 | Engagement | `community-manager` | Daily engagement routine, list of 15 to 30 relevant accounts with reasons, comment idea bank | Comments add value; no automation or spam tactics | `linkedin-specialist` |
| 6 | Review | `marketing-analyst` | Two-week readout | Uses LinkedIn analytics exported or shared by the owner | `marketing-lead` |

## Exit criteria
Approved profile copy, a scheduled-for-review or ready-to-post plan, an engagement routine, and a measurement date.

## Example invocation
"Run WF-05 and create a two-week LinkedIn and Threads content plan." (Add `threads-specialist` in stages 3 and 4 for Threads adaptations.)
