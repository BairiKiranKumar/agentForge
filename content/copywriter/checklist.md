# Copywriter: Checklist

## Before starting
- [ ] Page type and the one primary action confirmed
- [ ] Customer problem available in their own words (reviews, enquiries, owner notes)
- [ ] Offer facts collected from the client or rate card (services, timings, location, inclusions)
- [ ] Proof list with sources and permission references
- [ ] Voice guide read if it exists
- [ ] Character budgets per slot received from `conversion-designer` or the layout
- [ ] Diagnosis stated: A (blank or generic), B (real voice) or C (point edit)

## While working
- [ ] Every line passes the three-question gate or is flagged `[NEED: ...]`
- [ ] Claims table filled: claim, source, status, unlock evidence
- [ ] Superlatives ("best", "No.1", "only") blocked unless independently substantiated
- [ ] No health cure, guaranteed admission, guaranteed ranking or income claims
- [ ] Prices and discounts copied exactly from the rate card or client brief
- [ ] Testimonials are real words with recorded consent
- [ ] In Situation B, the owner's key phrases preserved and listed
- [ ] Hero headline fits two lines at 375 px
- [ ] CTA labels use a verb plus outcome; WhatsApp CTAs say what happens next
- [ ] Sentences mostly under 20 words; no idioms a second-language reader may miss
- [ ] Indian formats used: ₹ with Indian grouping, 10 am to 8 pm, enquiry, colour
- [ ] Ad copy within current platform limits (verified on the help page, date noted)

## Before handoff
- [ ] Search done for em dashes: zero found
- [ ] Search done for AI tells: contrast reveals, negation lists, colon reveals, "The result?", stock openers
- [ ] Leave-it-alone test applied; unjustified changes reverted
- [ ] At least 2 headline and 2 CTA alternatives with reasons
- [ ] Meta title and description drafts within length
- [ ] `[NEED]` list sent to the owner or the client
- [ ] Assumptions, open questions and risks listed
- [ ] Handoff written in `orchestration/handoff-format.md` format with evidence

## Stop and escalate if
- [ ] The client insists on a claim with no evidence
- [ ] Copy compares with a named competitor
- [ ] Copy is for a regulated claim area (medical, education results, finance)
