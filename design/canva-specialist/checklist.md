# Canva Specialist: Checklist

## Before starting
- [ ] Brief has platform, format, number of designs and deadline
- [ ] Copy is approved final wording, not a draft
- [ ] Brand kit found in Canva, or brand guidelines with logo, hex colours and fonts
- [ ] Every image has a source and rights status
- [ ] Canva connector availability checked and the account and plan known
- [ ] Brand templates or past approved designs for this format searched first

## While working
- [ ] Build path chosen (brand template, copy of approved design, new)
- [ ] Slot inventory written: every text and image slot mapped to an approved asset
- [ ] Slot and asset mismatches resolved with the owner before generation
- [ ] Each upload confirmed successful before it is referenced
- [ ] One design built and previewed at about 360 px wide before the series
- [ ] Body text at least 28 px and headlines 64 px or more on 1080 px canvases
- [ ] Text contrast checked at 4.5:1 and recorded
- [ ] Text kept out of story and Reel UI overlay zones
- [ ] Two fonts maximum, from the brand kit; Telugu in a script-supporting font
- [ ] Prices, dates and phone numbers match the approved copy exactly
- [ ] Platform size verified on the platform's help page, with date
- [ ] One design at a time; stopped and reported on a second rate-limit error

## Before handoff
- [ ] Every export opened and checked for placeholders, sample text, overflow and font fallback
- [ ] Files named `<client>-<campaign>-<format>-v<n>` and placed in the campaign folder
- [ ] Build log updated with export paths and verification status
- [ ] Rights entry recorded for every uploaded image
- [ ] High-visibility pieces sent to `design-reviewer`
- [ ] Assumptions, open questions and risks listed
- [ ] Handoff written in `orchestration/handoff-format.md` format with evidence

## Stop and escalate if
- [ ] You are asked to publish, schedule or share a design publicly
- [ ] An image's rights are unknown or it shows people without consent
- [ ] The job needs paid elements or a plan upgrade
- [ ] Copy does not fit and would need rewording
