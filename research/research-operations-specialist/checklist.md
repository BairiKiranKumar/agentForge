# Research Operations Specialist: Checklist

## Before starting
- [ ] Study plan approved by `research-lead` with criteria, quotas, mode, language, dates
- [ ] Incentive amount approved by the owner, or "no incentive" recorded
- [ ] Client agreed to contact with its customers and named who sends the first message
- [ ] Storage location, access list and retention period agreed
- [ ] Consent template reviewed by `privacy-specialist` for sensitive topics

## While working
- [ ] Screener asks about recent behavior, not interest or identity
- [ ] Study topic hidden until the end of the screener
- [ ] Terminate condition written for each must-have criterion
- [ ] Age gate excludes anyone under 18
- [ ] Screener collects only selection and scheduling data
- [ ] Participants from studies in the last 3 months excluded
- [ ] At least two recruiting channels when the owner hand-picks participants
- [ ] Group posts made only with the admin's permission; replies one-to-one
- [ ] Outreach text approved by the owner before sending
- [ ] IDs assigned at screening; names and numbers only in `_private/`
- [ ] Consent notice sent in the participant's language before the session
- [ ] Consent and recording consent recorded separately with dates
- [ ] Reminders 24 hours and 1 hour before; backup per session day
- [ ] Over-recruited by about 20 to 25% (30% for diaries)

## Before handoff
- [ ] Incentives paid within 48 hours, references logged, no UPI IDs stored
- [ ] Notes and shared files contain IDs only
- [ ] Show rate recorded
- [ ] Retention date set and deletion log started
- [ ] Assumptions, open questions and risks listed
- [ ] Handoff written in `orchestration/handoff-format.md` format with evidence

## Stop and escalate if
- [ ] Any participant may be under 18
- [ ] Someone asks for recordings or contact details outside the repository
- [ ] The client's customer list was collected for another purpose and customers were not told
