# Case Study Writer: Examples

Business names below are fictional unless stated.

## Example 1: Play school website case study (named)
**Request:** "Write a case study on the Little Steps Play School site. The owner is happy."

**Key decisions:**
- Permission: owner approved "Named" on WhatsApp but asked that no children appear; recorded as Named without children's photos.
- Interview in Telugu-English by phone (Kiran ran it from the guide). Owner said "calls are much more"; asked for data. She shared WhatsApp Business label counts: 18 enquiries in September, 41 in November.
- November is admission season; noted as another change, wording uses "after".

**Output (excerpt):**
```
Permission: Named, no children's images (WhatsApp, 2026-11-20, approved v3)

Snapshot
Client: Little Steps Play School, Kompally, Hyderabad
Built: Mobile-first website with WhatsApp enquiry button and visit booking
Timeline: 3 weeks, September 2026
Verified result: WhatsApp enquiries 18 (September) to 41 (November), owner's WhatsApp Business labels. Admissions season began in November.

## Before
"Parents would see our Instagram, then call, and if I was in class I missed it," says Lakshmi, the founder.

Results log:
| Statement | Baseline | After | Source | Other changes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| WhatsApp enquiries | 18 (Sep 2026) | 41 (Nov 2026) | owner label counts, screenshot 2026-11-19 | admission season; no ads |
```

## Example 2: Anonymised clinic booking app
**Request:** "The clinic won't be named, but we want a case study for proposals."

**Key decisions:**
- Permission: Internal only for proposals; clinic does not want public use.
- Screenshots retaken with seeded patients ("Test Patient 01"); real appointment screens excluded.
- No outcome data; result section describes what changed in the reception workflow from the receptionist's quote.

**Output (excerpt):**
```
Permission: Internal only (email from clinic manager, 2026-10-30)
Proposal insert (about 120 words):
A physiotherapy clinic in Hyderabad was booking appointments in a paper register and on calls. We built a booking app with a reception view, patient reminders by email and a daily schedule screen. The receptionist told us: "I don't have to flip pages when a patient calls." The app was delivered in 5 weeks on Next.js and Supabase, with role-based access so only reception and doctors see patient details.
(No metrics: the clinic has not shared appointment data.)
```

## Example 3: Boundary: "Round it up to 3x"
**Request:** "The restaurant's orders went from 120 to 260 a week. Just say orders tripled, it sounds better."

**Response (excerpt):** "I can't write 'tripled': 120 to 260 is a little over double, and the owner would be quoted beside a wrong number. Also, the period included Dussehra week, which I've noted. Suggested line: 'Weekly table orders went from 120 to 260 in the six weeks after QR ordering launched (owner's POS export; includes Dussehra week).' If the owner shares a later month without a festival, we can update the number then."
