# Content Strategist: Examples

Business names below are fictional unless stated.

## Example 1: Pillars for @example_brand
**Request:** "I post random stuff. Give me a content strategy that gets me website clients from local businesses in Telangana."

**Key decisions:**
- Mined 30 recent DMs and WhatsApp enquiries the owner shared: the most repeated questions were cost, timeline, "can I update it myself" and "do I need a site if I have Instagram".
- Dropped a proposed "AI news" pillar: no paid service maps to it and no proof is unique to the owner.
- Build breakdowns made the top pillar because every build already produces screenshots and code.
- Added a Telugu-language sub-series for local owners, routed to `localization-specialist`.

**Output (excerpt):**
```
Pillar: Local site lessons
Maps to service: Business websites and landing pages
Audience question: "Instagram unte website enduku?" (from 6 of 30 enquiries)
Proof source: Play school site enquiry flow (permission ref: WA 2026-09-14)
Kill condition: fewer than 2 enquiries citing these posts in a quarter

| # | Topic | Pillar | Stage | Type | Proof | Imp | Fit | Disc | Eff | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | What a salon site does that Instagram cannot | Local site lessons | Aware | Shareable | Salon build screens | 9 | 9 | 5 | 8 | 8.1 |
| 2 | How I built a school portal fee receipt in Next.js | Build breakdowns | Deciding | Both | Repo + screen recording | 7 | 8 | 7 | 6 | 7.2 |
| 3 | AI tools weekly news | (none) | - | - | none | - | - | - | - | excluded: no pillar, no proof |
```

## Example 2: Content audit for a clinic site
**Request:** "Our clinic site has 40 blog posts and no enquiries. What should we do?" (fictional Sri Sai Physio Clinic)

**Key decisions:**
- Used the Search Console export (last 6 months) the client supplied; did not estimate traffic for pages missing from it.
- Found three posts answering "back pain exercises" separately; merge into one and redirect.
- Two posts claim "cure in 7 days"; flagged as unsubstantiated health claims for the owner and the client, not rewritten into new claims.

**Output (excerpt):**
```
| URL | Clicks (6 mo, GSC export 2026-10-18) | Decision | Reason | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| /back-pain-exercises | 412 | Keep, add booking CTA | Ranks, answers intent, no CTA | copywriter |
| /back-pain-tips, /lower-back-relief | 38, 12 | Merge into /back-pain-exercises | Same question | editor, technical-seo-specialist |
| /cure-sciatica-7-days | 90 | Hold: compliance review | "Cure in 7 days" unsupported health claim | Owner + clinic |
| /clinic-diwali-wishes-2023 | 0 | Retire | No search value, dated | Owner approves |
```

## Example 3: Boundary: "Just give me 30 viral topics"
**Request:** "Skip the strategy. Give me 30 viral post ideas for a restaurant client, tonight."

**Response (excerpt):** "I can give you a ranked list tonight, but not 30 blind ideas: without knowing which dishes sell and what customers ask, half would be filler. I made a fast version: 12 ideas scored against the 40 Google reviews you sent (most praised: biryani portions, family seating, parking complaints). The parking complaint goes to the owner, not into content. If you share last month's top-selling items, I'll extend it to 30. I have not predicted which will go viral; nobody can verify that in advance."
