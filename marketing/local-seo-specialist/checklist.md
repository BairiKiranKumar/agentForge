# Local SEO Specialist: Checklist

## Before starting
- [ ] Legal business name confirmed against the signboard and registration documents
- [ ] Trading name, if different, recorded and marked as such
- [ ] Physical address or service-area status confirmed in writing
- [ ] Customer-facing phone number confirmed as local (not toll-free)
- [ ] Owner of the Google account identified; manager access requested by invitation only
- [ ] Existing listings (Google, Justdial, Sulekha, Facebook and others relevant) recorded with their details
- [ ] Any move, rename or merger in the last 12 months recorded with the date

## Profile
- [ ] Primary category is the most specific match to the services offered
- [ ] Secondary categories match only services actually offered
- [ ] Business name has no keywords added and matches the registration
- [ ] Address, phone and website identical across profile, website footer and contact page
- [ ] Hours current, including holiday and lunch closures, confirmed by owner
- [ ] Each service listed with a short description
- [ ] Description under 750 characters, primary service and town in the first sentence, no URLs or superlatives
- [ ] Logo, cover photo and at least 10 real photos of premises, team or work uploaded
- [ ] Question list seeded with 5 to 10 real questions and answers

## Citations and NAP
- [ ] Each relevant directory checked and marked correct, wrong, missing or duplicate
- [ ] Duplicate and wrong listings corrected through the directory's own claim process
- [ ] NAP correction sheet kept with date fixed for each row
- [ ] Recheck scheduled at 30 days

## Reviews and replies
- [ ] Review request method is open to all customers, with no incentive
- [ ] Review reply templates drafted in the customer's language, under 60 words
- [ ] Negative reply drafts acknowledge the experience, offer an offline route and do not argue facts
- [ ] Reply target of 48 hours recorded in the report

## Area pages (only if the business travels to customers)
- [ ] Each area page is for an area the business actually serves, with the reason stated
- [ ] Each area page has at least 300 words of unique, verifiable content
- [ ] No more pages than the business can serve well; near-duplicate pages merged

## Handoff and stop
- [ ] Every profile change backed by owner confirmation in writing
- [ ] Monthly report includes the query, location, device and date for any rank observation
- [ ] Stop and escalate on any request for fake, bought or incentivised reviews
- [ ] Stop and escalate on any duplicate merge or listing removal before submission
- [ ] Stop and escalate on any change to a registered name, address or GST-linked detail
