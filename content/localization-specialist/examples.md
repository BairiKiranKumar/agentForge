# Localization Specialist: Examples

Business names below are fictional unless stated. Telugu lines in these examples are illustrative drafts and would still need the native-speaker check before use.

## Example 1: Salon Instagram captions: script choice
**Request:** "Make Telugu versions of next week's 4 captions for Glow Studio, Nizamabad."

**Key decisions:**
- Counted the last 20 customer comments on the account: 14 Romanized Telugu or code-mixed, 4 English, 2 Telugu script. Chose Romanized code-mix for captions.
- Kept "bridal", "trial" and "booking" in English: the owner and customers use them.
- Glossary spelling: "cheyyandi" (not "cheyandi").
- Checker: the salon's senior stylist (Telangana Telugu speaker).

**Output (excerpt):**
```
Piece: SAL-041 caption | Source: EN v2 "Book your bridal trial this week. Slots fill fast in wedding season."
Mode: transcreate | Script: Romanized code-mix | Register: friendly, "meeru" forms
Telugu (Romanized): "Ee week mee bridal trial book cheyyandi. Wedding season lo slots twaraga full avthayi."
Note: "fill fast" rendered as "twaraga full avthayi", the way customers say it; "pelli season" considered, owner prefers "wedding season".
Native-speaker check: Ramya (senior stylist, Nizamabad), 2026-10-21, v1, changed "e vaaram" to "ee week" | Status: ready
```

## Example 2: School admission notice in Telugu script
**Request:** "Translate the admission notice for Sunflower Kids for the website and a printed banner."

**Key decisions:**
- Mode: translate (dates, ages, documents must be exact). Script: Telugu script for both.
- Fees kept as digits with ₹; dates as digits with Telugu month names checked by the reviewer.
- Banner exported from Canva with Noto Sans Telugu; first export split a conjunct in the school's name; fixed by switching the text box font and re-exporting.
- "Birth certificate" kept in English beside the Telugu term because parents use both (glossary entry).

**Output (excerpt):**
```
| # | English | Telugu | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Admissions open for 2027 to 2028 | 2027 నుండి 2028 అడ్మిషన్లు ప్రారంభం | "అడ్మిషన్లు" kept as parents say it |
| 2 | Age: 2 to 5 years | వయస్సు: 2 నుండి 5 సంవత్సరాలు | Translated exactly |
Rendering: Canva export v2 checked on Android, conjuncts correct (v1 broken, fixed)
Native-speaker check: school office manager, Hyderabad, 2026-10-21, v2, one month name corrected | Status: ready
```
