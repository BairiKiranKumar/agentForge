# Business Strategist: Examples

Business names below are fictional unless stated. Figures are illustrative inputs, not real prices.

## Example 1: Which segment to focus on next quarter
**Request:** "Should I focus on schools, clinics or restaurants for the next 6 months?"

**Key decisions:**
- Decision: one beachhead segment for portfolio examples, content and outreach until April 2027.
- Scored with evidence: 2 delivered school projects (proof), 1 clinic, 0 restaurants but a QR ordering prototype.
- Contribution per founder hour from the owner's last projects: school portals higher value but more founder hours; clinic sites smaller, faster, repeatable.

**Output (excerpt):**
```
Decision: beachhead segment for Nov 2026 to Apr 2027 | Decide by: 2026-10-31
Recommendation: clinics (dental and physiotherapy) in Telangana, with schools as the second segment
| Segment | Pain x freq | Pay | Reach | Proof | Repeat | Competition | Weighted |
| Clinics | 4 | 4 | 3 [Inference] | 2 | 4 | 3 | 3.4 |
| Schools | 3 | 3 | 4 | 4 | 3 | 3 | 3.35 |
| Restaurants | 4 | 2 [Inference] | 3 | 1 | 4 | 2 | 2.75 |
(Competition column is reversed: higher = less crowded. Schools and clinics are close; proof for clinics is the weakest input, so B1 tests it.)
Not doing: restaurant outreach and the QR ordering product until April review; no new sectors
Bet B1: "Clinic owners will buy a booking-first site package" | test: 5 owner conversations + demo | success: 2 ask for a proposal | kill: 0 of 5 | time box: 4 weeks | 8 founder hours
Risk: clinic patient data under DPDP Act 2023; booking forms collect minimum data (route to privacy-specialist)
```

## Example 2: Productized package vs custom projects
**Request:** "Should I sell a fixed salon website package instead of custom quotes?"

**Key decisions:**
- Compared custom project vs package on contribution per founder hour using the owner's 3 salon projects: custom averaged 22 founder hours; a template could cut that to about 8 `[Inference]`.
- Package needs a template, strict scope and a price from `pricing-strategist`.

**Output (excerpt):**
| Option | Rs. per client | Founder hrs | Contribution per founder hr | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Custom (status quo) | rate card avg | 22 (measured) | baseline | slow, feast-famine |
| Package | hypothesis from pricing-strategist | 8 [Inference] | about 2x baseline [Inference] | clients wanting customization |
Not doing: custom salon builds under the package price.

## Example 3: Pushback: "launch all three product ideas"
**Request:** "Let's launch the QR print-shop app, the restaurant ordering app and the school app this quarter."

**Response (excerpt):** "With about 10 studio hours a week and active client work, three product launches would each get roughly a few hours a week, which is not enough to learn anything. I recommend one bet at a time with a 6-week box. The school app has the most proof (two school clients) and the clearest buyer, so test that first: 5 school owner conversations and a pilot offer. The print-shop and restaurant ideas go on the 'not doing' list until the April review, with a note on what evidence would bring each back."
