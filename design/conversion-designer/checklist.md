# Conversion Designer: Checklist

## Before starting
- [ ] One conversion goal named, with what happens after it and who responds within what time
- [ ] Each traffic source listed with a sample ad, post or search term
- [ ] Offer details supplied: services, published prices or "from" prices, timings, location
- [ ] Proof inventory built with permission and date for each item
- [ ] Analytics window (14 to 30 days) and device split obtained, or marked missing

## While working
- [ ] Primary and secondary actions chosen from the intent table, one primary style per screen
- [ ] Headline slot matches each traffic source's promise
- [ ] Above the fold at 360 x 740 shows what, for whom, where, primary CTA and one trust cue
- [ ] Sections ordered as an argument: promise, proof, how it works, offer, objections, action
- [ ] CTA repeated after proof and after objections, not in every section
- [ ] Each form field has a recorded reason; default enquiry form 3 fields or fewer
- [ ] Mobile number field accepts +91 and spaces, validates 10 digits, numeric keyboard
- [ ] Chips or radios used for under 5 options
- [ ] Consent and purpose line next to submit, wording requested from `privacy-specialist`
- [ ] Thank-you state says who, when and which channel
- [ ] Trust numbers exact and dated; testimonials consented
- [ ] No fake urgency, pre-ticked boxes, confirmshaming or hidden costs
- [ ] Sticky Call/WhatsApp bar specified with safe-area padding and hidden while typing
- [ ] Hero has no video or carousel blocking LCP

## Before handoff
- [ ] Copy slots specified for `copywriter` with intent and length
- [ ] Event list for every CTA tap and form step handed to `web-analytics-specialist`
- [ ] Test-worthy ideas handed to `conversion-optimization-specialist`, not presented as certain wins
- [ ] Audit (if any) has at most 7 ranked fixes and a "could not check" list
- [ ] Assumptions, open questions and risks listed
- [ ] Handoff written in `orchestration/handoff-format.md` format with evidence

## Stop and escalate if
- [ ] The client asks for fabricated or inflated proof
- [ ] The form would collect health, child or identity data without `privacy-specialist` review
- [ ] Nobody is assigned to respond to enquiries
- [ ] You are asked to promise a conversion lift
