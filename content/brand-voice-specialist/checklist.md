# Brand Voice Specialist: Checklist

## Before starting
- [ ] At least 10 voice samples collected (messages, captions, review replies, call notes)
- [ ] Customer personal data redacted from samples
- [ ] Positioning and audience inputs located, or their absence recorded
- [ ] Channels and languages per channel listed
- [ ] Owner's likes and dislikes collected
- [ ] For the owner's brand, company-profile voice section read as baseline

## While working (guide)
- [ ] Voice constants and tone flexes written separately
- [ ] At least 4 we are / we are not rows with evidence and example lines
- [ ] Account placed on each attribute spectrum with a sample line
- [ ] Tone matrix covers at least 3 real contexts with sample lines
- [ ] Word list has use, avoid (with replacement) and banned tiers
- [ ] Company-profile bans included: corporate filler, hype, em dashes
- [ ] Indian English decisions recorded ("revert", "kindly", "do the needful")
- [ ] Code-mixing rule set per channel with `localization-specialist`
- [ ] Confidence level on every rule; low-confidence rules turned into open questions with recommendations

## While working (audit)
- [ ] Guide version stated in the audit
- [ ] Findings logged with location, rule, severity and before/after
- [ ] Implied claims checked against evidence ("premium", "luxury", "best")
- [ ] Typos and number errors left to `proofreader`
- [ ] Rewrites beyond targeted fixes returned to the writer

## Before handoff
- [ ] Verdict given: on voice, minor drift, off voice
- [ ] No High finding without a fix
- [ ] Guide versioned and changelog updated
- [ ] Word list shared with `proofreader`
- [ ] Assumptions, open questions and risks listed
- [ ] Handoff written in `orchestration/handoff-format.md` format with evidence

## Stop and escalate if
- [ ] The client asks to imitate a real competitor's distinctive voice or tagline
- [ ] Samples include unredacted personal data or recordings without consent
- [ ] A voice change would alter positioning (route to `brand-strategist`)
