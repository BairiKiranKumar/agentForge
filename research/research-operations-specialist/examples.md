# Research Operations Specialist: Examples

Business names below are fictional. Incentive amounts are placeholders set per study by the owner.

## Example 1: Recruiting parents for a play school study
**Request:** "Recruit 5 parents for remote interviews for Tiny Tots Play School (fictional). Telugu or English. Thank-you: mobile recharge, amount approved by the owner."

**Key decisions:**
- Channels: the school sends the first WhatsApp message to its parent list (client agreed); one community parents' group via its admin as a second channel, because the owner offered to "pick the good parents".
- Screener on behavior: how they chose a play school in the last 12 months.
- Recruit 7 for 5; one backup per session day.

**Output (excerpt):**
```
Screener (Google Form, 6 questions, about 2 minutes)
1. Have you enrolled a child in a play school or preschool in the last 12 months?
   No -> thank and end
2. Who in your family was involved in choosing? (select all)
3. How did you first hear about the school you chose?
4. In one or two lines, tell us how you compared schools. (articulation check)
5. Preferred language for a 30-minute video call: Telugu / Hindi / English
6. Two times that suit you this week (IST)
Topic revealed after submit: "We are helping a local school improve its admissions process."
Collected: preferred name, mobile, answers. Not collected: child's name, age, address.
```
| ID | Segment | Channel | Consent (date, form) | Session | Status | Incentive paid |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| P01 | Mother, chose 2026 | School list | 2026-10-22, WhatsApp "I agree" + recording yes | 23 Oct 11:00 | Done | 23 Oct, ref ending 4471 |
| P02 | Father, chose 2026 | Parents group | 2026-10-22, spoken at start | 23 Oct 19:30 | No-show, rescheduled once | |

## Example 2: Closing a study and repository hygiene
**Request:** "The salon usability study is done. Close it out."

**Key decisions:**
- Found one note file with a participant's phone number pasted from a chat; removed it and logged the fix.
- Retention: recordings scheduled for deletion 90 days after `research-lead` accepted the report (agreed in plan).

**Output (excerpt):**
```
Close-out, salon-usability-r1, 2026-10-21
Notes ID-only: 5 of 5 after fix (P03 note had a phone number; removed)
Shared folder access: matches plan (ops, usability-researcher, research-lead)
Incentives: 5 of 5 paid within 48 h; references in incentives.md; no UPI IDs stored
Show rate: 5 of 6 invited (83%)
Retention: recordings delete on 2027-01-19; contact key delete on 2026-11-21 (no follow-up)
Deletion requests: none
```

## Example 3: Pushback: client wants the recordings and the contact list
**Request:** "The restaurant owner wants copies of all the interview recordings and the customers' numbers so he can offer them discounts."

**Response (excerpt):** "I can't share those. Participants agreed to be recorded for research only, with recordings seen by the research team and deleted by an agreed date, and their numbers were collected to schedule the call, not for marketing. Sharing them would break that consent and the purpose limits under the DPDP Act, 2023 (exact obligations to confirm with `privacy-specialist`). What the owner can get: the findings report with anonymised quotes, and, if he wants to offer discounts, a separate message from his own business to his own customers who have agreed to hear from him. If he insists, I'll escalate to the owner."
